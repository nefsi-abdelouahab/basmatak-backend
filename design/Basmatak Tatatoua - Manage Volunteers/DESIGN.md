## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a disciplined, civic-minded, and professional volunteer ecosystem. Its personality balances high-utility networking with human empathy, avoiding the ephemeral, dopamine-driven trends of hyper-gamified social apps or the sterile, clinical appearance of enterprise SaaS software. It emphasizes credibility, trust, clear information density, and community impact.

### Guiding Principles
- **Restraint Over Decoration:** Zero decorative blobs, frivolous gradients, or extraneous floating badges. Visual weight is reserved strictly for high-value user content, verified organizational achievements, and clear calls to action.
- **Civic Seriousness:** The visual tone respects the integrity of civil service, non-governmental initiatives, and grassroots volunteer coordination.
- **Human Usability:** Content consumption remains effortless through predictable vertical rhythms, deliberate typographic contrast, and robust support for bilingual parity (Arabic and English).
- **Subtle Modernism:** A hybrid design philosophy blending the structured architecture of LinkedIn with the direct, readable feed mechanics of classical social platforms, executed with pristine line work, crisp border contours, and flat surfaces.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around functional discipline. Primary Violet serves as the signature marker of civic leadership, intentional interaction, and focused brand presence, anchored on a neutral canvas engineered for prolonged readability and reduced visual fatigue.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Violet (`#6D28D9`):** Interactive primary triggers, key active navigation tabs, active state indicators, and focal interactive elements.
- **Primary Hover (`#5B21B6`):** Hover and pressed states for primary triggers and dense buttons.
- **Tint / Light Violet (`#F5F3FF`):** Active nav row fills, contextual highlights, and subtle focus backdrops.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Global viewport background, providing soft contrast against pure white cards.
- **Surface Layer (`#FFFFFF`):** High-density cards, structured content feeds, fixed sidebars, and dialogue modals.
- **Text Primary (`#111827`):** High-contrast headline and body typography, ensuring absolute legibility.
- **Text Secondary (`#64748B`):** Metadata, timestamps, engagement counts, muted role indicators, and inactive tab labels.
- **Structural Border (`#E2E8F0`):** 1px structural framing for standard panels, card perimeters, input outlines, and dividers.
- **Subtle Border (`#F1F5F9`):** Secondary nested dividers, table rows, and inner component separations.

### Functional Status Indicators
Functional colors are applied strictly to communicate semantic truth rather than aesthetic flair:
- **Success (`#16A34A`):** Exclusively reserved for confirmed institutional verifications, completed volunteer commitments, and positive status markers.
- **Warning / Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Pending applications, action-required notifications, and limited-seat alerts.
- **Error (`#DC2626`):** Destructive actions, validation alerts, and service disruptions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes rapid scannability, structural clarity, and multi-script harmony. Letterforms are grounded, legible, and completely devoid of expressive display styling.

### Typographic Specifications
- **Latin Script (Primary):** Set in `Inter` across all structural UI hierarchies, employing systematic optical alignment and balanced metrics.
- **Arabic Script (Secondary / Dual-Primary):** Paired dynamically with `IBM Plex Sans Arabic`. The scale maintains equivalent visual density and x-height alignment without requiring manual spacing offsets.
- **Weight Governance:** Restricted to `400` (Regular) for body and contextual narrative, `500` (Medium) for interactive triggers, tabs, and metadata labels, and `600` (Semi-bold) for headlines and identity titles. Arbitrary heavy weights (e.g., 800/900) are prohibited.
- **Readability Rules:** Core post bodies are locked strictly to 14px and 15px with relaxed line heights (`1.5` to `1.53`) to accommodate long-form organizational manifests and mission recaps comfortably.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows a fixed 3-column architecture calibrated against a primary desktop viewport of 1440px with a max container width of 1280px, anchored in human-centered information architecture.

### Grid Architecture (1440px Desktop Anchor)
- **Global Structure:** Centered container (max-width: 1280px) flanked by auto margins.
- **Left Column (Navigation & Profile Context):** 240px fixed width. Sticky top positioning (`top: 80px`). Hosts global routing, identity shortcuts, and core civic action links.
- **Center Column (Main Activity Feed):** ~700px fluid-capped width. Hosts the post composer, organizational updates, active initiative streams, and discussions.
- **Right Column (Contextual & Institutional Modules):** ~300px fixed width. Sticky top positioning (`top: 80px`). Hosts upcoming volunteer opportunities, verified entity highlights, and civic guidelines.
- **Structural Gutters:** Fixed 24px (`1.5rem`) gap separating the three columns.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Reflow
- **Desktop Large (≥ 1280px):** 3-column expanded layout (240px | ~700px | 300px).
- **Tablet / Small Desktop (1024px – 1279px):** 2-column layout. The Right column drops out of the immediate grid and nests into contextual sub-drawers or beneath the main stream. Feed width expands to fill the remaining area.
- **Mobile (≤ 768px):** Single-column layout (100% viewport width minus 16px margins). Left navigation converts into a fixed bottom navigation bar (56px height) for critical paths; secondary modules are accessible via top-level tab switches.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, saturated glows, and pseudo-3D skeuomorphism. Elevation is expressed strictly through subtle tonal separation and hairline boundaries, maximizing perceived performance and stability.

### Depth Stratification
- **Base Canvas:** The foundation sits flat at `#F8FAFC`.
- **Card Surfaces:** Primary feed units and sidebars use pure `#FFFFFF` resting on the canvas, bounded by a continuous 1px outline of `#E2E8F0`.
- **Hairline Elevation (`shadow-sm`):** Permitted only on resting interactive cards: `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`.
- **Raised Elevation (Dropdowns, Floating Overlays):** Used for navigation menus, context menus, and tooltips: `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05)`.
- **Modal Backdrops:** Non-blurred, muted overlay of `#111827` at 40% opacity (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.40)`), centering a crisp 1px bordered `#FFFFFF` frame with elevated shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The geometric form language maintains an approachable yet rigorous character. Elements use balanced, non-exaggerated corners that avoid both utilitarian brutalism and toy-like hyper-rounding.

### Radius System
- **Core Radius (`rounded-xl` / 12px):** Default standard for feed cards, composer panels, profile summary modules, and dialogue surfaces.
- **Input & Trigger Radius (`rounded-lg` / 8px):** Standard for form inputs, primary/secondary action buttons, dropdown items, and media previews.
- **Micro Radius (`rounded-md` / 6px):** Checkbox containers, inline chips, status pills, and tooltips.
- **Circular (`rounded-full`):** Strictly reserved for user avatars, verified status icon rounds, and compact icon-only action triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background `#6D28D9`, Text `#FFFFFF`, font weight 500, height 40px, padding horizontal 16px, radius 8px (`rounded-lg`). Hover: `#5B21B6`. Active: `#4C1D95`. Focus: 2px offset ring in `#6D28D9`.
- **Secondary Button:** Background `#FFFFFF`, Border 1px `#E2E8F0`, Text `#111827`, font weight 500, height 40px. Hover: Background `#F8FAFC`, Border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost / Subtle Button:** Background transparent, Text `#64748B`. Hover: Background `#F1F5F9`, Text `#111827`.

### Tags & Status Pills
- **Standard Tag / Pill:** Restrained neutral appearance. Background `#F1F5F9`, Text `#475569`, border none or subtle 1px `#E2E8F0`. Padding 4px 10px, radius 6px (`rounded-md`), typography `label-sm`. No bright or saturated category pills.
- **Status Indicator (e.g., Opportunity Open):** Background `#F0FDF4`, Text `#16A34A`, border 1px `#DCFCE7`.

### Verification Badge
- **Entity & Contributor Badge:** A subtle, inline 14px checkmark icon rendered in Success Green (`#16A34A`). Paired inline with account title: `margin-inline-start: 4px`. Avoids complex multi-colored badges.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 40px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E2E8F0`, radius 8px, text `#111827`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Focus: border color `#6D28D9` with a 1px ring in `rgba(109, 40, 217, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Size 18px x 18px, border 1.5px `#CBD5E1`, radius 4px (checkbox) or circular (radio). Checked: `#6D28D9` fill with white checkmark.

### Feed Cards
- **Structure:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border `#E2E8F0`, radius 12px (`rounded-xl`), hairline shadow `shadow-sm`.
- **Padding:** Header, content body, and engagement actions are aligned with 16px internal padding.
- **Divider Lines:** Inner horizontal separation lines between the content and interaction bar are 1px solid `#F1F5F9`.
- **Engagement Bar:** Restrained text-and-icon triggers for support, comments, and volunteer registration, maintaining `#64748B` with transition to `#6D28D9` on interaction.

### Internationalization & RTL Readiness
- **Bi-directional Mirroring:** All components rely on logical properties (`margin-inline-start`, `padding-inline-end`, `inset-inline-start`).
- **Typography Swap:** When viewing in Arabic, the layout shifts to RTL reading order seamlessly, replacing `Inter` with `IBM Plex Sans Arabic` while retaining identical typographic scale tokens and vertical baselines.
