About this Opportunity

In direct municipal collaboration with the Wilaya Cultural Directorate of Adrar and the Maison de la Culture de Timimoun, this civic mission aims to capture, transcribe, and permanently archive the 2026 annual gathering of the traditional Ahellil poetic-musical tradition. Originating from the historic earthen settlements of the Gourara region, Ahellil holds recognized status on UNESCO’s Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

The selected civic volunteer team will be stationed at the historic red mud-brick Old Ksar (القصر القديم بتيميمون) alongside veteran oral historians. Volunteers will document master reciters, choreographic formations, and vernacular architectural settings under rigorous non-commercial open-access standards.

All captured photographic plates, uncompressed field audio reels, and transcribed indices will be formally indexed into the Algerian National Civic Archival Portfolio. Participating volunteers will receive official ministerial civic credit hours verified under Executive Decree conditions governing Law 12-06.

Primary Civic Mission Deliverables
  • check Creation of a public domain photographic register documenting 14 master elders and traditional instrumentalists.
  • check Six uncompressed 96kHz/24-bit field audio master recordings captured during evening communal performances.
  • check Bilingual accession logbook mapping recital stanzas into classical Arabic and standardized Zenati transliteration.
