About this opportunity

The Gourara region harbors centuries of vernacular earthen architecture, intricate underground Foggara irrigation networks, and distinctive Ahellil polyphonic poetry. The annual Timimoun Cultural Heritage Gathering brings master builders, oral historians, and desert artisans together inside the historic fortified Ksar.

Our volunteer media team serves as the civic chronicle of this gathering. As an event documentation volunteer, you will work closely alongside local cultural custodians to capture high-resolution imagery and moving footage that will be deposited into the National Intangible Heritage Civic Archive and published across regional educational platforms.

You will be matched with an assigned coordinator from the Timimoun Cultural Association to ensure unhindered stage access, proper respectful protocol during prayer ceremonies and artisan demonstrations, and seamless delivery of your captured media assets.
