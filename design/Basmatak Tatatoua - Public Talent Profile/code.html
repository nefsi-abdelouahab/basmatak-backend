article About & Field Focus

SEC.01

Abdelouahab bridges software development (Next.js, Python, GIS data systems) with cultural media storytelling in southern Algeria, specifically focusing on the Timimoun and Gourara oasis regions. His work addresses technical equity for non-governmental initiatives, creating robust bilingual interfaces that serve both grassroots organizers and international research consortia.

He has been deeply committed to documenting indigenous engineering systems, notably the ancient Foggara subterranean water networks, translating physical water distribution registers into open geospatial databases. Beyond technical delivery, Abdelouahab organizes community workshops designed to mentor oasis youth in open-source web technologies and collaborative civic mapping.
