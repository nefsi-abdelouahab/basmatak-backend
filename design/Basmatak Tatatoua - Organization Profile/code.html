About the Organization

The Timimoun Cultural Association (Association Culturelle de Timimoun) acts as an institutional and civil preservation bridge across the Gourara basin. Legally accredited under Algerian Civil Society Law 12-06, our mandate combines rigorous ethnolinguistic documentation with architectural conservation. Through continuous partnership with regional artisan guilds, traditional water masters (Keggadïn of the foggara networks), and academic researchers, we coordinate targeted civil volunteer cohorts to secure vulnerable intangible and built heritage.

Every volunteer initiative coordinated through our civic workspace adheres to formal archiving standards, ensuring that raw ethnographic recordings, architectural surveys, and oral poetry transcripts are safely deposited into national open-access repositories and verified through cryptographically authenticated civic experience records.
