About the Organization

Adrar Civic Registration

Founded in 2018, the Timimoun Cultural Association (جمعية تيميمون الثقافية) operates as an accredited civil society organization dedicated to the structural preservation, anthropological recording, and public curation of Saharan vernacular architecture, historic earthen ksar structures, and millennial foggara underground hydraulic canal networks across the Gourara region.

Under our statutory civic mandate, we coordinate structured youth field missions pairing university researchers, architectural trainees, and citizen volunteers with seasoned village artisans. Our primary civic programs safeguard the non-material UNESCO-inscribed Ahellil oral poetry traditions and maintain comprehensive geospatial condition registries for high-risk red-clay ksur across the wilaya of Adrar.
