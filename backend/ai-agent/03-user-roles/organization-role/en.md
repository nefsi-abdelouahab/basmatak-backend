# The Organization Role

An Organization is an association or organization that uses the platform to manage its volunteering needs.

## What an Organization Can Do

- Create an Organization account and a public organization profile.
- Publish, edit, close, or cancel volunteering opportunities.
- Define one or more positions in an opportunity, each with a required number of volunteers, required skills, deliverables, and a start time.
- Receive volunteer applications and review them.
- Accept or reject applications. A rejection requires a reason.
- Follow participation and remove a participant with a reason.
- Review submitted contributions and verify or reject them. A rejection requires a reason, and verification can include notes.
- Write a review of a volunteer's contribution: a rating from 1 to 5 and an optional comment.
- Publish its own email and phone on its public profile.
- See its public reliability: response rate and median decision time.

## Publishing Limit

An organization may be temporarily blocked from publishing new opportunities when too many of its opportunities have contributions waiting for review. The default threshold is 3 and it is configurable. Publishing unlocks after the pending contributions are reviewed.

## Important Decisions

- One opportunity can contain several positions, and each position can accept several volunteers.
- The platform does not use an "organization members" model.
- Only verified, active organizations are public.

## Organization Journey

1. Create an organization account and profile.
2. Publish an opportunity with its positions and requirements.
3. Receive applications.
4. Review applicants and accept or reject them.
5. Follow the volunteers' participation.
6. Review submitted contributions and verify or reject them.
7. The verified contribution becomes a Verified Experience for the volunteer.

## Why the Organization's Confirmation Matters

The value of a Verified Experience comes from the organization confirming that the work was really done.

## Common Questions

**What can an organization do on the platform?**
Publish and manage opportunities, review applications, manage participants, review contributions, verify work, and review volunteers.

**Can an opportunity accept more than one volunteer?**
Yes. Each position has its own required number of volunteers.

**Can I add several team members to my organization account?**
The platform does not use an organization members model in its current design.

**How do I verify a volunteer's contribution?**
Review the submitted contribution and confirm it if it was actually completed. You can add notes, and you must give a reason if you reject it.

**Why can't my organization publish a new opportunity?**
There may be too many contributions waiting for review. Review them to unlock publishing.

**Can an organization contact a volunteer directly?**
There is no messaging. The organization can use rejection or removal reasons, verification notes, review comments, and status notifications. It cannot see the volunteer's email or phone.
