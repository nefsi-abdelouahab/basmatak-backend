# The Organization Role

An Organization is an association or organization that uses the platform to manage its volunteering needs.

## What an Organization Can Do
- Create an Organization account.
- Create an organization profile.
- Publish volunteering opportunities.
- Describe needs and tasks.
- Receive volunteer applications.
- Review applicants.
- Select suitable volunteers.
- Follow task execution.
- Review submitted contributions.
- Confirm contributions that were actually completed.
- Give an evaluation of a contribution.

## Important Decisions
- One opportunity can accommodate several volunteers, not only one.
- The platform does not use an "organization members" model.

## Organization Journey
1. Create an organization account and profile.
2. Publish an opportunity describing the need and required skills.
3. Receive applications.
4. Review applicants and select volunteers.
5. Follow the volunteers' work.
6. Review submitted contributions.
7. Confirm completed contributions so they become Verified Experiences.

## Why the Organization's Confirmation Matters
The value of a Verified Experience comes from the organization confirming that the work was really done.

## Common Questions
**What can an organization do on the platform?**
Publish opportunities, review applications, select volunteers, follow tasks, review contributions, and confirm completed work.

**Can an opportunity accept more than one volunteer?**
Yes.

**Can I add several team members to my organization account?**
The platform does not use an organization members model in its current design.

**How do I verify a volunteer's contribution?**
Review the submitted contribution (description and evidence) and confirm it if it was actually completed.
