# The Visitor

A visitor is anyone using the platform without an account or without being signed in. Visitor is not an account type.

## What a Visitor Can Do

- Explore published volunteering opportunities and their full details.
- Browse public organization profiles.
- Browse public volunteer profiles and talents.
- View public Verified Experiences.
- Read public pages and the platform's public information.

## What a Visitor Cannot Do

- Apply to a position.
- Publish an opportunity.
- Submit a contribution.
- Manage a profile or portfolio.
- See private user data.
- Use Basma as a signed-in account. Basma is public and has no sign-in session.

## Why Visitors Matter

The platform is not only for volunteers and organizations. It also lets anyone discover skills, contributions, and verified volunteering work.

## Common Questions

**Can I browse the platform without registering?**
Yes. Public content is available to visitors.

**Can a visitor see private information about volunteers?**
No. Only public content is shown.

**Can I apply to an opportunity without an account?**
No. You can view the opportunity, but applying requires a Volunteer account.

**How do I become a volunteer?**
Create an account, choose the Volunteer account type, and sign in.

**I represent an association. What should I do?**
Create an account and choose the Organization account type.
