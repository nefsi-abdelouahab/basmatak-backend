# The Visitor

A Visitor is not an account type. It is anyone using the platform without an account or without being signed in.

## What a Visitor Can Do
- Explore volunteering opportunities.
- Browse public talents and public volunteer profiles.
- View public verified experiences.

## What a Visitor Cannot Do
- Apply to opportunities.
- Publish opportunities.
- Submit contributions.
- Manage a profile.
- See private user data.

To do any of these, the person needs to create an account (Volunteer or Organization) and sign in.

## Why Visitors Matter
The platform does not serve only volunteers and organizations. It also lets anyone discover skills and volunteering contributions.

## Common Questions
**Can I browse the platform without signing up?**
Yes, public content is available to visitors.

**Can a visitor see private information about volunteers?**
No. Only public content is shown.

**How do I go from visitor to volunteer?**
Create a Volunteer account and sign in.
