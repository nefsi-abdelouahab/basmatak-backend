# Basmatak Tatatoua Privacy, Reporting, and Safety Policies

## Privacy Overview

Basmatak Tatatoua respects users' privacy and strives to protect the information they provide when using the platform. The platform does not sell users' personal information. This privacy policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, when it may be shared, and what users can request regarding their personal data.

## Information We Collect

The information collected may include usernames, account information, profile pictures, information displayed on public profiles, contact information voluntarily provided by users, volunteering opportunities, applications, contributions, and photos, videos, or files attached to contributions.

It may also include technical information necessary to operate, maintain, and improve the platform.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Allow volunteers to apply for opportunities.
- Manage volunteering opportunities and contributions.
- Verify experiences after confirmation by the relevant organization or beneficiary.
- Display information that users choose to make public.
- Protect the platform and prevent misuse.
- Maintain and improve the platform's services.

## When Information May Be Shared

Information may be shared when necessary to operate the service, when required by law, or when necessary to protect the rights and security of the platform and its users.

The platform does not sell users' personal information.

## Public Information and Media

Some information may be publicly visible, particularly details displayed on volunteer public profiles and verified experiences.

Users should avoid making sensitive or personal information public unless they are comfortable sharing it.

When uploading photos, videos, or other files, users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights and permissions to use and share that content.

## Data Retention and User Rights

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the service or fulfill legal obligations.

Users may request access to their personal information, corrections, deletion of specific information, or deletion of their accounts, subject to the platform's capabilities and applicable laws.

No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## User Rights

Every user has the right to:

- Know how their personal information is used.
- Access their personal information and request corrections.
- Control the information displayed on their public profile, subject to platform capabilities.
- Request account or personal information deletion, subject to applicable laws and platform capabilities.
- Report inappropriate content or misconduct.
- Be treated fairly and without discrimination or abuse.
- Contact the platform administration when facing problems or violations of their rights.

## User Responsibilities

All Basmatak Tatatoua users are expected to:

- Provide accurate information and avoid impersonating others.
- Respect volunteers, organizations, and other platform users.
- Avoid using the platform for illegal activities.
- Avoid publishing other people's personal information without permission.
- Never submit forged certificates, documents, or evidence.
- Respect intellectual property rights.
- Never attempt to access accounts or information they are not authorized to access.
- Report suspicious activities or violations they encounter.

## Reporting Inappropriate Content

Registered users can report content that violates the platform's rules.

The platform administration reviews submitted reports and may review or remove content that violates the platform's terms, applicable laws, or policies.

The platform may also suspend or terminate accounts that violate these terms or applicable laws.

Users must not include another person's personal information in a report without permission.

## Types of Reports

Reports may concern the following situations:

- Impersonation of a volunteer or organization.
- Fake or misleading volunteering opportunities.
- Forged documents or fabricated evidence.
- Abusive, offensive, or illegal content.
- Harassment, threats, or abuse directed at users.
- Misleading commercial or personal use of the platform.
- Privacy violations or unauthorized disclosure of personal information.
- Suspected fraud or exploitation.
- Any other behavior that violates platform rules.

## Report Handling Process

The Basmatak Tatatoua administration reviews reports according to the following process:

1. Receive the report and the related information.
2. Review the reported content or behavior.
3. Assess the severity of the violation and examine the available evidence.
4. Request additional information when necessary.
5. Take appropriate action based on the nature of the violation.
6. Inform the relevant parties of the outcome when appropriate and possible.

Reports are handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Submitting a report does not automatically mean that the reported person or organization has been found guilty of a violation.

## Violation Measures and Penalties

Depending on the nature, severity, and recurrence of a violation, the platform administration may take one or more of the following actions:

- Issue a warning or notice to the user.
- Request modification or removal of violating content.
- Remove content that violates platform rules.
- Temporarily restrict certain account features.
- Temporarily suspend an account.
- Permanently terminate an account in serious or repeated cases.
- Suspend an organization's ability to publish opportunities or revoke its verification when justified by confirmed violations.

Actions are determined based on the available evidence, taking into consideration the proportionality between the violation and the measure applied.

## Right to Appeal Decisions

If a user believes that an action taken by the platform administration was incorrect or unfair, they may contact the administration through the platform's official communication channels and request a review.

The appeal should explain the decision being challenged and provide the reasons supporting the request.

The administration reviews the appeal based on the available information and may confirm, modify, or reverse the original decision.

## Resolving Disputes Between Volunteers and Organizations

Disagreements may arise during volunteering activities, including disputes about tasks, responsibilities, commitments, contribution evaluations, or evidence acceptance.

In such cases:

- Volunteers and organizations are encouraged to communicate directly and respectfully to resolve the disagreement.
- Either party may contact the platform administration if a resolution cannot be reached.
- The administration may review available information and evidence to understand the nature of the dispute.
- The platform does not interfere in an organization's internal decisions except within the scope of its responsibilities and policies.
- Rejection of a contribution for verification alone does not automatically constitute misconduct unless other supporting evidence exists.

## Content and Safety Rules

Content published on the platform must not include illegal or abusive material, personal information belonging to others without permission, impersonated or misleading content, or material that violates intellectual property rights.

Users must not submit false information, fabricated images or documents, or claim other people's work as their own.

When submitted evidence includes photos or videos, volunteers must confirm that they have permission to share them.

## Volunteer Safety During Activities

Basmatak Tatatoua encourages volunteers and organizations to follow appropriate safety practices during volunteering activities:

- Review the opportunity description and required tasks before participating.
- Make sure the organizing entity and the nature of the activity are clear.
- Follow the organization's instructions and safety guidelines.
- Avoid performing tasks that pose unreasonable risks to health or safety.
- Never share passwords or sensitive financial or personal information with others.
- Inform the organization about unsafe conditions or practices during an activity.
- Seek assistance from the appropriate authorities in situations involving immediate danger.

The platform does not replace the organization's responsibility to organize its activities and provide appropriate and safe participation conditions.

## Organization Responsibilities

Registered organizations are responsible for:

- Providing accurate information about their identity and activities.
- Publishing genuine and clearly described volunteering opportunities.
- Explaining tasks, requirements, duration, location, and participation format.
- Avoiding the use of volunteers for illegal or misleading activities.
- Respecting volunteers' privacy and avoiding misuse of their personal information.
- Reviewing submitted contributions and evidence fairly and objectively.
- Avoiding requests for unnecessary personal information or documents.
- Treating all volunteers respectfully.

Platform approval of an organization's verification does not constitute an absolute guarantee of all its future activities or behavior.

## Accounts and Organizations

Users must provide accurate information when creating accounts, keep their login credentials confidential, avoid using another person's account or creating accounts under false identities, and use the platform for lawful purposes.

Registered organizations are responsible for the accuracy of the volunteering opportunities they publish.

Only verified organizations are allowed to publish opportunities.

## Account Security

To help protect their accounts, users are encouraged to:

- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Never share login credentials with anyone.
- Log out when using shared devices.
- Avoid opening suspicious links claiming to belong to the platform.
- Notify the administration if they suspect their account has been compromised.
- Update their account information when important changes occur.

The platform administration will never ask users to share their passwords.

## Security Incident Handling

If a user suspects an account compromise, information leak, or unauthorized activity, they should notify the platform administration through its official communication channels.

Users should provide a description of the issue without sharing passwords or confidential login information.

The administration reviews the report and takes appropriate action based on the nature of the incident and the available technical capabilities.

## Role of the AI Assistant in Privacy and Safety

The AI Assistant within Basmatak Tatatoua is a support and guidance tool that follows these principles:

- Provide information based on the platform's official policies and documentation.
- Never fabricate information about users or organizations.
- Never disclose private account information or personal data.
- Never share one user's information with another user.
- Never make administrative decisions, such as verifying organizations, accepting applications, or imposing penalties.
- Direct users to the platform administration when a problem requires human intervention.
- Never promise specific outcomes for reports or appeals.
- Never present its responses as a replacement for official administrative decisions.

## Availability and Updates

The platform strives to remain available and reliable. However, service interruptions, maintenance periods, and changes may occur.

The terms of use and privacy policy may be updated. New versions will be published along with their update dates.

For questions regarding these policies, users should contact the platform through its official communication channels.

## Platform Responsibility and Scope

Basmatak Tatatoua operates as a digital platform connecting volunteers with organizations and volunteering opportunities. It provides tools for managing participation and documenting contributions.

The platform is not a direct party to every volunteering activity and does not guarantee the quality of every opportunity or the behavior of every user or organization.

Each party remains responsible for its own actions and obligations under applicable laws.
