# Glossary of Platform Terms

## Volunteer
A user who looks for volunteering opportunities, applies, carries out tasks, and submits contributions. Arabic: متطوع.

## Organization
An association or organization that publishes volunteering opportunities, reviews applications, selects volunteers, and confirms contributions. Arabic: جمعية أو منظمة.

## Visitor
Anyone using the platform without an account or without being signed in. A visitor is not an account type. Visitors can only explore public content (opportunities, public profiles, public verified experiences). Arabic: زائر (شخص غير مسجّل الدخول).

## Opportunity
A volunteering opportunity published by an organization to describe a need it wants volunteers to fulfill. One opportunity can accommodate several volunteers. Arabic: فرصة تطوعية.

## Application
A volunteer's request to join an opportunity. Sending an application does not make the volunteer a participant. Arabic: طلب تقديم / طلب مشاركة.

## Selection
The step where the organization reviews applications and chooses the volunteers it approves.

## Participation
A volunteer's actual involvement in carrying out an opportunity after being selected. Arabic: مشاركة.

## Task
The work a selected volunteer carries out within an opportunity. Arabic: مهمة.

## Contribution / Submission
What the volunteer sends after finishing the work: a description of what they did, with evidence such as photos or videos. Arabic: مساهمة / إرسال المساهمة.

## Evidence
Material attached to a contribution to show the work was done, such as photos or videos. Arabic: دليل / أدلة.

## Review
The organization's examination of a submitted contribution. The organization can also give an evaluation. Arabic: مراجعة / تقييم.

## Verification
The organization's confirmation that the contribution was actually completed. Arabic: التحقق / التوثيق.

## Verified Experience
A volunteering contribution that the organization has reviewed and confirmed. It appears on the volunteer's public profile. Arabic: تجربة تطوعية موثقة.

## Skill
An ability a volunteer lists on their profile, or a skill an opportunity requires. Arabic: مهارة.

## Public Profile
The profile that others can explore. Its key content is the volunteer's verified experiences. Arabic: الملف العام.

## My Profile
The user's own profile where they manage their information and content. Arabic: ملفي الشخصي.

## Discover Talents
A page for discovering volunteers and talents on the platform by skills and experience. Arabic: اكتشاف المواهب.

## Dashboard
The main page the user sees after signing in. Its content depends on the user's role. Arabic: لوحة التحكم.

## Common Questions
**What is the difference between a Contribution and a Verified Experience?**
A contribution is what the volunteer submits. It becomes a Verified Experience only after the organization confirms it.

**Is "Application" the same as "Participation"?**
No. Application is the request to join. Participation is the actual involvement after selection.
