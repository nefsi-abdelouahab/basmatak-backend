# Glossary of Platform Terms

## Volunteer

A user who looks for volunteering opportunities, applies, carries out tasks, and submits contributions. Arabic: متطوع.

## Organization

An association or organization that publishes volunteering opportunities, reviews applications, selects volunteers, and confirms contributions. Arabic: جمعية أو منظمة.

## Visitor

Anyone using the platform without an account or without being signed in. A visitor is not an account type. Visitors can explore public content: opportunities, public profiles, public organization profiles, and public verified experiences. Arabic: زائر (شخص غير مسجّل الدخول).

## Opportunity

A volunteering opportunity published by an organization to describe a need it wants volunteers to fulfill. One opportunity can contain several positions. Arabic: فرصة تطوعية.

## Position

A role inside an opportunity with its own title, description, required number of volunteers, required skills, deliverables, and start time. Arabic: منصب.

## Application

A volunteer's request to join a specific position in an opportunity. Sending an application does not make the volunteer a participant. Arabic: طلب تقديم / طلب مشاركة.

## Selection

The step where the organization reviews applications and chooses the volunteers it approves. Arabic: الاختيار.

## Participation

A volunteer's actual involvement in carrying out an opportunity after being accepted. Arabic: مشاركة.

## Task

The work a selected volunteer carries out within an opportunity. Arabic: مهمة.

## Contribution / Submission

What the volunteer sends after finishing the work: a title, a description of what they did, and evidence. Arabic: مساهمة / إرسال المساهمة.

## Evidence

Material attached to a contribution to show the work was done: JPEG, PNG, or WebP images, PDF files, and links to YouTube or Google Drive videos. Arabic: دليل / أدلة.

## Review

An organization's rating of a volunteer's contribution, from 1 to 5 with an optional comment. Arabic: مراجعة / تقييم.

## Verification

The organization's confirmation that the contribution was actually completed. Arabic: التحقق / التوثيق.

## Verification Code

A code given to a verified experience. Anyone can check it on the public verification page. Arabic: رمز التحقق.

## Verified Experience

A volunteering contribution that the organization has reviewed and confirmed. It can appear on the volunteer's public profile. Arabic: تجربة تطوعية موثقة.

## Skill

An ability a volunteer lists on their profile, or a skill an opportunity position requires. Arabic: مهارة.

## Portfolio

A volunteer's curated showcase of work items. It is independent of Verified Experiences and needs no verification. Arabic: ملف الأعمال.

## Public Profile

The profile that others can explore. Its key content is the volunteer's verified experiences. Arabic: الملف العام.

## My Profile

The user's own profile where they manage their information and content. Arabic: ملفي الشخصي.

## Discover Talents

A page for discovering volunteers and talents on the platform by skills and experience. Arabic: اكتشاف المواهب.

## Dashboard

The main page the user sees after signing in. Its content depends on the account type. Arabic: لوحة التحكم.

## Common Questions

**What is the difference between a Contribution and a Verified Experience?**
A contribution is what the volunteer submits. It becomes a Verified Experience only after the organization confirms it.

**Is "Application" the same as "Participation"?**
No. Application is the request to join. Participation is the actual involvement after selection.

**What is a position?**
A role inside an opportunity, with its own requirements and number of volunteers.
