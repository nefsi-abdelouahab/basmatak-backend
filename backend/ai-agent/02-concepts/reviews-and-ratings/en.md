# Reviews and Ratings

## What a Review Is

A review says how well a volunteer's work was done. It is different from verification, which says that the work was done.

## Who Writes a Review

Only the organization that owns the contribution can write a review of the volunteer's work. Volunteers do not review organizations.

## The Rating

- A rating from 1 to 5.
- An optional comment of up to 2000 characters.
- One review per contribution.

## When It Is Written

A review is written as part of the verification flow, when the organization confirms the contribution.

## Where It Appears

A visible review appears on the volunteer's public profile. A hidden review is excluded from public display and from reliability calculations.

## Reviews and Reliability

Organization reliability is computed from the organization's submissions, verifications, and visible reviews. A hidden review does not count.

## Common Questions

**Can a volunteer review an organization?**
No. Reviews are organization-to-volunteer only.

**What rating scale is used?**
1 to 5.

**Can I write a comment with the rating?**
Yes, up to 2000 characters.

**How many reviews can one contribution receive?**
One.

**Is the review public?**
A visible review appears on the volunteer's public profile.

**Does a review replace verification?**
No. A review says how well the work was done; verification says the work was done.

**Can a hidden review affect reliability?**
No. Hidden reviews are excluded from reliability calculations.
