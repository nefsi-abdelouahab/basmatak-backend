# Skills vs Proven Experience

## Two Different Things
- A skill listed on a profile is a claim by the volunteer.
- Proven experience comes from a verified contribution confirmed by an organization.

The platform keeps this distinction on purpose.

## Why There Are No Skill Badges
Skill badges were deliberately excluded from the first version. The goal is to avoid turning skills into symbols that do not prove the person really has them. Real experience is shown through verified contributions instead.

## How Skills Are Used
- Volunteers present their skills on their profile.
- Opportunities describe the skills they require.
- The platform uses skills to connect volunteers with suitable opportunities.
- The Discover Talents page lets people find volunteers by skills and experience.

## Examples of Skill Areas Organizations May Need
- Programming and web development.
- Design and graphics.
- Photography and video editing.
- Marketing and social media management.
- Translation and content writing.
- Organization and event management.
- Other fields.

## Common Questions
**Does listing a skill prove I have it?**
No. Listing a skill is a claim. Proof comes from a verified contribution where you used that skill.

**Are there skill badges?**
No. They were excluded from the first version.

**How can I prove my skills?**
By taking part in opportunities, submitting contributions, and getting them confirmed by organizations.

**Can organizations find volunteers by skill?**
Yes. The Discover Talents page supports discovering volunteers by skills and experience.
