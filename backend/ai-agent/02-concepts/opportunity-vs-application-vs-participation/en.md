# Opportunity vs Application vs Participation

These are three different entities in the platform. They should not be confused.

| Entity | What it is | Who creates it |
|---|---|---|
| Opportunity | The volunteering opportunity describing a need | The organization |
| Application | A volunteer's request to join the opportunity | The volunteer |
| Participation | The volunteer's actual involvement after being selected | Follows the organization's selection |

## Opportunity
Published by an organization. It describes the need the organization wants volunteers to fulfill. One opportunity can accommodate multiple volunteers.

## Application
After exploring an opportunity, a volunteer can send an application to join it. Sending an application does not automatically make the volunteer a participant.

## Participation
After the organization reviews applications and selects volunteers, a selected volunteer moves to carrying out the task. This is participation.

## The Flow
Opportunity published -> Volunteer applies (Application) -> Organization selects -> Volunteer participates (Participation) -> Volunteer submits a contribution -> Organization verifies.

## Why the Separation Matters
The platform focuses on actual contribution, not mere registration. The difference is between:
- A person who applied.
- A person who was selected.
- A person who actually participated.
- A person whose contribution was verified.

## Common Questions
**If I apply, am I automatically accepted?**
No. Applying only sends a request. The organization reviews applications and selects volunteers.

**Can one opportunity have several volunteers?**
Yes. One opportunity can accommodate multiple volunteers.

**Where can I follow my applications?**
On the Applications page.

**What is the difference between Application and Participation?**
Application is the request to join. Participation is the actual involvement in the activity after being selected.
