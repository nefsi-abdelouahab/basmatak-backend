# Organization Reliability and the Publish Gate

## What Reliability Is

Reliability is a set of neutral metrics computed from an organization's activity. It helps volunteers judge how consistently an organization responds and decides.

## The Public Metrics

- Submission response rate: the share of uploaded submissions that received a decision.
- Median decision time: the median time between a submission and the organization's decision.

Metrics with no data are omitted rather than shown as zero.

## Where It Appears

For a verified, active organization, public reliability metrics are available on the public organization profile. Only verified, active organizations have a public profile.

## The Publish Gate

The publish gate limits how many opportunities an organization can have with contributions waiting for review.

- It counts the distinct opportunities that have at least one submission in submitted or under review.
- The default threshold is 3 and it is configurable.
- When the count reaches the threshold, publishing a new opportunity is blocked until the pending contributions are reviewed.
- Draft submissions and decided submissions do not count.

## Why It Exists

The gate encourages organizations to review contributions before taking on more work. It protects volunteers from waiting too long for a decision.

## Common Questions

**What is organization reliability?**
Neutral metrics computed from the organization's submissions and decisions.

**Can I see an organization's reliability?**
Yes, for a verified, active organization on its public profile.

**What is the publish gate?**
A limit that blocks new publishing when too many of the organization's contributions are waiting for review.

**What is the default threshold?**
3.

**Does a draft submission count toward the gate?**
No. Only submitted or under-review submissions count.

**How does an organization unlock publishing?**
By reviewing the pending contributions so that fewer opportunities have pending submissions.

**Does one opportunity with several pending submissions count more than once?**
No. The gate counts distinct opportunities, not submissions.
