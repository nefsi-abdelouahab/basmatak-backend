# What is a Verified Experience?

## Definition

A Verified Experience is a volunteering contribution that was actually carried out and confirmed by the responsible organization. It is the central value of Basmatak Tatatoua.

## What a Verified Experience Can Show

- The volunteering activity.
- The organization that ran the activity.
- The nature of the volunteer's contribution.
- The evidence linked to the contribution.
- The verification status.
- The organization's review, when available.
- Organization notes, when available, shown to the volunteer and not publicly.

## Organization Notes

When the organization verifies a contribution, it can add notes of up to 2000 characters. The volunteer sees them as **Organization notes** on the verified experience card. The notes are not shown publicly.

## Verification Code and Attestation

A verified experience gets a verification code. Anyone can check it on the public verification page at /verify/<code>. A PDF attestation can be generated.

## Why It Matters

- It gives young people practical proof of their experience, more informative than a participation certificate or a count of hours.
- It lets others see what a person actually did and which skills they used.
- It helps organizations and visitors discover volunteers based on real work.

## How a Contribution Becomes a Verified Experience

1. An organization publishes an opportunity.
2. The volunteer applies and is accepted.
3. The volunteer carries out the task.
4. The volunteer submits a contribution with a description and evidence.
5. The organization reviews and confirms it.
6. It becomes a Verified Experience on the volunteer's public profile.

## The Core Equation

Skill + Volunteering Opportunity + Actual Contribution + Organization Confirmation = Verified Experience.

## Common Questions

**What is a Verified Experience?**
A volunteering contribution confirmed by the organization as actually completed.

**Where do Verified Experiences appear?**
On the volunteer's public profile and in the Verified Experiences page.

**Can anyone see my Verified Experiences?**
They are shown on the public profile when the experiences setting is on, which is the default. See the volunteer public settings topic.

**Can the organization leave notes when verifying?**
Yes. The organization can add notes of up to 2000 characters when it verifies a contribution.

**Where do I see the organization's notes?**
On the verified experience card, under **Organization notes**. They are visible to you and are not shown publicly.

**Is a Verified Experience a certificate?**
It is more than a certificate. It records the work, the skills used, the evidence, and the organization's confirmation. A PDF attestation can also be generated.

**How can someone check my verified experience?**
With its verification code on the public verification page at /verify/<code>.
