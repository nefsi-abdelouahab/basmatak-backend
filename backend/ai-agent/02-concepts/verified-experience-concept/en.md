# What is a Verified Experience?

## Definition
A Verified Experience is a volunteering contribution that was actually carried out and confirmed by the responsible organization. It is the central value of Basmatak Tatatoua.

## What a Verified Experience Can Show
- The volunteering activity.
- The organization that ran the activity.
- The nature of the volunteer's contribution.
- The evidence linked to the contribution.
- The verification status.
- The evaluation, when available.

## Why It Matters
- It gives young people practical proof of their experience, more informative than a participation certificate or a count of hours.
- It lets others see what a person actually did and which skills they used.
- It helps organizations and visitors discover volunteers based on real work.

## How a Contribution Becomes a Verified Experience
1. An organization publishes an opportunity.
2. The volunteer applies and is selected.
3. The volunteer carries out the task.
4. The volunteer submits a contribution with description and evidence.
5. The organization reviews and confirms it.
6. It becomes a Verified Experience on the volunteer's public profile.

## The Core Equation
Skill + Volunteering Opportunity + Actual Contribution + Organization Confirmation = Verified Experience.

## Common Questions
**What is a Verified Experience?**
A volunteering contribution confirmed by the organization as actually completed.

**Where do Verified Experiences appear?**
On the volunteer's public profile and in the Verified Experiences page.

**Can anyone see my Verified Experiences?**
They are meant to be shown on the public profile. For exact privacy settings, see the public vs private data topic.

**Is a Verified Experience a certificate?**
It is more than a certificate. It records the work, the skills used, the evidence, and the organization's confirmation.
