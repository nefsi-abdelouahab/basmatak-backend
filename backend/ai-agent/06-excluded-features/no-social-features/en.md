# No Social Features: Followers, Likes, and Popularity Ranking

## What the Platform Does Not Have
- No followers.
- No likes.
- No popularity ranking.
- No social popularity system.

## Why
Basmatak Tatatoua is not designed to become a social network whose goal is collecting followers and likes. The value it highlights is real contribution. The number of followers or likes does not prove that someone did volunteering work.

## What the Platform Focuses On Instead
- Skills.
- Contributions.
- Evidence.
- Organization confirmation.
- Verified Experiences.

## How People Are Discovered
In Discover Talents, people are found through their skills and verified experiences, not through popularity or interaction counts.

## Common Questions
**Can I follow other volunteers or organizations?**
No. The platform has no followers feature.

**Can I like a profile or a contribution?**
No. There are no likes.

**Is there a ranking of top volunteers?**
No. There is no popularity ranking. People are discovered by skills and verified experience.

**How can I stand out then?**
By taking part in opportunities, submitting contributions, and getting them verified by organizations.
