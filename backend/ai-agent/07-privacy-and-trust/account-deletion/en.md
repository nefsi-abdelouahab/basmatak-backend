# Account Deletion

## Availability

Both account types can delete their account from the platform settings. Deletion does not require contacting the project team.

## What Happens

- The account is soft-deleted: it is marked as deleted rather than physically removed.
- All sessions for the account are revoked.
- Signing in is no longer possible.
- A deleted organization is removed from public view.
- A deleted volunteer's public profile is no longer shown.

## What Is Not Available

Data export is not available. There is no download of account data.

## Retention

I do not have confirmed information about how long deleted account data is retained or when it is permanently removed.

## Before Deleting

Consider that:

- Deletion ends access to the account and its sessions.
- A deleted account cannot be restored by signing in again.
- Verified Experiences are linked to the volunteer's record. I do not have confirmed information about whether they remain visible after deletion.

## Common Questions

**Can I delete my account?**
Yes. Account deletion is available for both account types.

**Is deletion permanent?**
The account is soft-deleted and all sessions are revoked. I do not have confirmed information about the permanent removal schedule.

**Will I be signed out?**
Yes. All sessions are revoked.

**Can I export my data first?**
No. Data export is not available.

**Can I restore a deleted account?**
I do not have confirmed information about restoring a deleted account. Signing in is no longer possible after deletion.

**What happens to my Verified Experiences?**
I do not have confirmed information about how Verified Experiences are handled after account deletion.

**Can an organization delete its account?**
Yes. A deleted organization is removed from public view.
