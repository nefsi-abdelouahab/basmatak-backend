# Terms of Use Summary

## Status of This Topic
The full text of the platform's Terms of Use is not included in this knowledge base. This topic only states rules that are confirmed from the platform's design. Do not invent legal clauses.

## Confirmed Rules from the Platform's Design
- An account type (Volunteer or Organization) is chosen at registration and cannot be changed later. A new account is needed.
- Only organizations can publish opportunities.
- Only volunteers can apply to opportunities and submit contributions.
- A contribution becomes a Verified Experience only after the responsible organization confirms it.
- A volunteer cannot verify their own contribution.
- Visitors can only view public content.

## How the Agent Should Answer
For questions about legal terms (liability, prohibited content, account suspension, intellectual property, age requirements), say that the detailed Terms of Use are not available to the assistant and direct the user to the Terms link in the platform footer or to the Roeya Association contact details.

## Common Questions
**Where can I read the Terms of Use?**
In the Terms link in the platform footer.

**What happens if someone submits a false contribution?**
I do not have confirmed information about penalties. The platform's design relies on organization verification, so an unconfirmed contribution does not become a Verified Experience.

**Who owns the content I upload?**
I do not have confirmed information about this. Please check the Terms page.

**Is there a minimum age to join?**
I do not have confirmed information about age requirements.
