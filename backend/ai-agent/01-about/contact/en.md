## Contacting an Organization

An organization's public profile shows its email to everyone. The organization's phone is shown only to signed-in users. Basma cannot display an organization's email or phone, so open the organization's public profile to see the contact details available to you. Volunteers can also use the "Email the organization" link on the Submit Contribution page. The platform has no messaging system.
