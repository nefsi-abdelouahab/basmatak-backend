# Hackathon Context

Basmatak Tatatoua was developed as part of the participation in the National Hackathon for Innovation in Volunteer Work 2026 (Algeria).

The project is linked to the team of the Roeya Association from Timimoun.

## Common Questions
**Why was the platform created?**
It was developed in the context of the National Hackathon for Innovation in Volunteer Work 2026, to propose an innovative digital solution for volunteering.

**Is it a hackathon project only?**
The platform was built in the hackathon context. This information does not cover future plans beyond what is described in the roadmap.
