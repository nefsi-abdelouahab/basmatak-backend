# Vision and Mission

## Vision
To build a digital platform that makes volunteering a way to gain and prove practical experience, connects the abilities of young people with the needs of associations, and creates a documented record of volunteering contributions.

## Mission
To make volunteering opportunities easier to reach, and to improve organizational communication between volunteers and associations through a digital system that documents actual contributions and turns them into trustworthy experiences.

## Core Value
From Volunteering to Verified Experience.

## Long-term Direction
- Phase 1: a digital volunteering platform that manages the full volunteering cycle: opportunities, applications, volunteer selection, participation, contributions, verification, and verified experiences.
- Phase 2: an AI-powered volunteering platform where users can interact with the platform using natural language through an AI assistant.

## Common Questions
**What is the platform's vision?**
To make volunteering a way to build and prove real experience.

**What is the platform's mission?**
To simplify access to volunteering opportunities and document real contributions as trustworthy experiences.
