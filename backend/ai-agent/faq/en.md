# Basmatak frequently asked questions

## What is Basmatak?

Basmatak Tatatoua is a digital platform in Algeria that connects volunteers with volunteering opportunities and helps associations and organizations find people for their initiatives. It focuses on documenting what volunteers actually accomplish and turning confirmed contributions into verified volunteer experiences.

## Who can use Basmatak?

Volunteers can discover opportunities, apply, participate, and document their contribution. Non-profit associations and organizations can publish opportunities, review applications, and confirm completed work. Public visitors can browse published opportunities, verified active organizations, and public volunteer profiles.

## How do I create an account?

Register as a volunteer or as an organization. Provide accurate information and keep your login credentials secure. Depending on the platform settings, you may need to confirm your email address before applying, publishing, or submitting evidence. Until the account is confirmed, it can browse only. An organization account remains pending until an administrator approves the organization's verification, and the organization cannot publish before that approval.

## What does verification mean?

Verification has two meanings on Basmatak.

- **Organization verification:** the platform reviews an organization and allows it to publish opportunities once approved.
- **Experience verification:** after a volunteer completes a task and submits evidence, the organization reviews the work and confirms that it was completed. Only then does the contribution become a verified experience.

## How do I find opportunities?

Open the opportunities feed and search by title, organization, skill, or place. Filter by wilaya, category, required skills, and format. The available formats are on-site, remote, and hybrid. You can sort by newest, oldest, closing soon, or starting soon. Each opportunity page shows its description, positions, prerequisites, deliverables, dates, and application deadline.

## How do I apply for an opportunity?

Sign in with a volunteer account, open the opportunity, choose a position with an open seat, and apply before the deadline. One active application per position is allowed. Applying stops at the application deadline. You can withdraw a pending or accepted application.

## What happens after I apply?

The organization reviews applications and can accept or reject them. If you are accepted, you become a participant. At the opportunity start date, accepted participants become active automatically. After completing the work, you submit evidence. The organization verifies or rejects the submission. If it is rejected, you can edit and resubmit it.

## How are verified experiences used?

A verified experience is a record of confirmed volunteer work. It is kept with the volunteer's experiences and can be shown on a public profile when the volunteer chooses. The platform describes verified records as proof that can be used in CVs and university applications.

## How do organizations publish opportunities?

Register an organization account and complete organization verification. After the organization is approved, create an opportunity with its positions and publish it. Only verified organizations can publish. Opportunities move through published, in-progress, and completed states, or can be closed or cancelled.

## What appears on a public profile?

Volunteers control their public profile. They can enable or disable the profile, choose which sections to show, and decide whether an individual verified experience is public. Public visitors can browse public profiles, but private contact details are not shown publicly.

## How is privacy handled?

The platform does not sell personal information. It uses information to manage accounts, applications, contributions, and verification, to display what the user chooses to make public, to protect the platform, and to improve the service. Information may be shared when necessary to operate the service, when required by law, or to protect rights and security. Users can request access, correction, or deletion through the platform's official contact channels, subject to platform capabilities and applicable law.

## How do I report content or a safety concern?

Signed-in users can report content that violates the platform rules. Reports are reviewed by the platform administration. Do not publish another person's personal information without permission. For urgent safety concerns, use the platform's official contact channels.

## What content is not allowed?

The platform does not allow illegal or abusive content, another person's personal information without permission, impersonation or misleading content, intellectual-property infringement, false information or documents, or claiming another person's work. When submitted evidence includes images or video, the volunteer must confirm that there is consent to share them.

## How can I get help?

Use the official contact channels published on the platform. The assistant cannot share private contact details, account data, or another person's information.

## Is Basmatak Tatatoua free to use?**

Users can browse volunteering opportunities and access the platform's available services according to their account permissions. For more details about specific services, please refer to the information published on the platform.

## Can I apply for multiple volunteering opportunities at the same time?**

Yes. You can apply for different opportunities simultaneously. However, only one active application is allowed per position within the same opportunity.

## Can I edit my application after submitting it?**

You cannot directly edit an application after submission. You can withdraw it if it is pending or accepted, then apply again if the opportunity is still open and the requirements are met.

## What does it mean when my application is pending?**

It means that the organization has received your application but has not made a decision yet. You must wait for the organization to review it and either accept or reject it.

## Can I participate in remote volunteering opportunities?**

Yes. The platform offers different participation formats: on-site, remote, and hybrid. You can use the search filters to find opportunities that match your preferred format.

## What happens when the application deadline passes?**

New applications are no longer accepted once the specified deadline has passed. We recommend applying before the deadline.

## Can I withdraw after my application has been accepted?**

You can withdraw an accepted application in situations supported by the platform. If your participation has already become active, we recommend contacting the organization to inform it of any changes.

## What is the difference between participation and verified experience?**

Participation means that your application has been accepted and you have taken part in a volunteering opportunity.

Verified experience is a contribution that you have actually completed, submitted evidence for, and had reviewed and confirmed by the organization.

## Is experience verified automatically when an opportunity ends?**

No. Completing an opportunity does not automatically verify your experience. You must submit the required evidence, which the organization will review before confirming the completed work.

## What should I do if the organization rejects my submitted evidence?**

You can review the reason for rejection, modify your evidence or provide the missing information, and resubmit it for review.

## Can I hide my verified experiences?**

Yes. You can control the visibility of your public profile and decide whether individual verified experiences should be visible to visitors.

## Can an organization publish opportunities immediately after registering?**

No. The organization must first complete the verification process and receive approval from the platform administration before it can publish opportunities.

## Can visitors apply for volunteering opportunities?**

No. Visitors can browse opportunities and public information, but applying requires signing in with a volunteer account.

## Can an organization reject my application without explaining why?**

Organizations are responsible for reviewing applications and deciding whether to accept or reject them. You can check your application status through your account and contact the organization if necessary.

## Can I report a suspicious organization or volunteering opportunity?**

You can use the reporting mechanism available on the platform to notify the administration about suspicious content or behavior. The administration will review the report and take appropriate action.

## Does the platform guarantee that I will get a job through my verified volunteering experiences?**

No. Basmatak Tatatoua helps you document your volunteering experiences and contributions, which you can use in your CV and academic or professional portfolio. However, it does not guarantee employment or university admission.
