# Volunteer FAQ

## Getting Started
**How do I join the platform as a volunteer?**
Create an account and choose the Volunteer account type at registration.

**Can I change my account type later?**
No. The account type is chosen at registration and cannot be changed. A new account is needed.

**What should I put in my profile?**
Your personal and professional information and the skills you have. Your public profile will also show your Verified Experiences.

**Is the platform free?**
I do not have confirmed information about pricing.

## Opportunities and Applications
**How do I find opportunities?**
On the Opportunities page. Open an opportunity to see its details.

**How do I apply to an opportunity?**
Open the opportunity details and send an application. You need to be signed in with a Volunteer account.

**Can I apply to several opportunities?**
I do not have confirmed information about limits on the number of applications.

**Where can I follow my applications?**
On the Applications page.

**Am I accepted automatically when I apply?**
No. The organization reviews applications and selects volunteers.

**What do I do after being selected?**
Carry out the task. You can follow it in My Tasks.

## Contributions and Verification
**I finished my task. What now?**
Submit your contribution from the Submit Contribution page with a description and evidence (photos or videos).

**Is my contribution counted immediately?**
No. It becomes a Verified Experience only after the organization reviews and confirms it.

**Who verifies my contribution?**
The organization responsible for the activity. You cannot verify it yourself.

**Where do my verified experiences appear?**
On your public profile and in the Verified Experiences page.

**Can I edit a contribution after submitting it?**
I do not have confirmed information about editing after submission.

**Are there file size limits for photos and videos?**
I do not have confirmed information about file limits.

## Profile and Skills
**Does listing a skill prove I have it?**
No. Listing a skill is a claim. Proof comes from a verified contribution.

**Are there skill badges, likes, or followers?**
No. The platform does not have them.

**Can others see my profile?**
Your public profile can be explored by others. I do not have confirmed details about visibility options.
