# Volunteer FAQ

## Getting Started

**How do I join the platform as a volunteer?**
Create an account and choose the Volunteer account type at registration.

**Can I change my account type later?**
No. The account type is chosen at registration and cannot be changed. A new account is needed.

**What should I put in my profile?**
Your personal and professional information and the skills you have. Your public profile can also show your Verified Experiences, portfolio, and statistics.

## Profile and Privacy

**What is shown on my public profile by default?**
Biography, skills, experiences, portfolio, and statistics. Avatar, location, and availability are hidden by default.

**Can I change what is public?**
Yes, from the public profile settings.

**Is my email or phone shown publicly?**
No. Email and phone are never shown on the public profile.

**Is my full name always shown?**
Yes.

## Opportunities and Applications

**How do I find opportunities?**
On the Opportunities page. Search by title or description, and filter by category, skill, wilaya, municipality, or open positions.

**How do I apply to an opportunity?**
Open the opportunity, choose a position with an open seat, and send an application. You need to be signed in with a Volunteer account.

**Can I apply to several opportunities?**
Yes. You can have one live application per position.

**Can I change my cover message after applying?**
No. The cover message is not editable after applying.

**Where can I follow my applications?**
On the Applications page.

**Am I accepted automatically?**
No. The organization reviews applications and decides.

**Can I withdraw an application?**
Yes, while it is pending or under review, and also after acceptance before the participation is completed.

## Tasks and Participation

**What happens after I am accepted?**
The task appears on the My Tasks page. When the opportunity has a start date, it starts automatically at that date.

**What do the participation states mean?**
Accepted means you were accepted and the task has not started. Active means the work is in progress. Completed means the contribution was verified. Withdrawn means you withdrew. Removed means the organization removed you with a reason.

**Can I withdraw after being accepted?**
Yes, before the participation is completed. This frees the seat.

## Contributions and Verification

**How do I submit a contribution?**
From the Submit Contribution page, add a title, a description, and evidence for each deliverable.

**What evidence can I attach?**
JPEG, PNG, or WebP images and PDF files up to 5 MB each, plus YouTube or Google Drive video links. Up to 50 items are allowed.

**Can I edit my contribution?**
Only while it is a draft or after it is rejected. Editing a rejected submission returns it to draft, and you can submit it again.

**Can I delete my contribution?**
No.

**Does submitting make it verified?**
No. The organization must review and confirm it.

**Who verifies my contribution?**
The organization responsible for the activity.

## Reviews, Portfolio, and Account

**Can I review an organization?**
No. Reviews are organization-to-volunteer only. You can receive a rating from 1 to 5 with an optional comment.

**What is the portfolio?**
A showcase of your work items. It is independent of Verified Experiences and needs no verification.

**How do I get a Verified Experience?**
After the organization verifies your contribution, it becomes a Verified Experience on your public profile.

**Can I delete my account?**
Yes. Account deletion is available and is a soft delete that ends all sessions.

**Can I export my data?**
No. Data export is not available.

**How do I receive notifications?**
Through the in-app notification center. Email is used only for account verification and password reset.
