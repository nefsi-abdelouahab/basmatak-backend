# Troubleshooting Common Situations

Important: this topic contains general guidance based on how the platform works. It does not describe specific error messages or technical fixes, which are not confirmed in this knowledge base.

## I applied but I do not see any task in My Tasks
Tasks appear after an organization selects you. Applying alone does not create a task. Check the Applications page for the state of your application.

## I submitted a contribution but it is not a Verified Experience
A submission becomes a Verified Experience only after the organization reviews and confirms it. Wait for the organization's review.

## I do not see any verified experiences on my profile
Verified Experiences appear only after organizations confirm your contributions.

## I cannot apply to an opportunity
Applying requires a signed-in Volunteer account. Visitors and Organization accounts cannot apply.

## I cannot publish an opportunity
Only Organization accounts can publish opportunities. If you registered as a volunteer, the account type cannot be changed, so a new Organization account is needed.

## I chose the wrong account type
The account type cannot be changed after registration. Create a new account with the right type.

## I cannot find the opportunity I want
Explore the Opportunities page. I do not have confirmed details about search and filter options.

## I cannot verify my own contribution
This is by design. Only the organization can verify contributions.

## I want to contact the organization
There is no messaging system in the first version. Use the organization's contact information where available.

## The platform is slow or takes time to respond
I do not have confirmed technical information. The platform uses free-tier hosting services, so occasional delays may happen, but I cannot confirm the cause. Try again after a moment.

## The assistant gave me an answer I think is wrong
The assistant answers from the platform's knowledge only. If it does not have confirmed information, it should say so. For anything important, check the relevant page in the platform or contact the Roeya Association.
