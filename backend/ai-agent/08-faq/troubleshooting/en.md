# Troubleshooting Common Situations

This topic gives general guidance based on how the platform works. It does not describe specific error messages or technical fixes.

## I applied but I do not see any task in My Tasks

Tasks appear after an organization accepts you. Applying alone does not create a task. Check the Applications page for the state of your application.

## I submitted a contribution but it is not a Verified Experience

A submission becomes a Verified Experience only after the organization reviews and confirms it. Wait for the organization's review.

## I do not see any Verified Experiences on my profile

Verified Experiences appear only after organizations confirm your contributions. Also check that the experiences setting is on in your public profile settings; it is on by default.

## I cannot apply to an opportunity

Applying requires a signed-in Volunteer account. Visitors and Organization accounts cannot apply. Check that the opportunity is still published, the deadline has not passed, and the position has an open seat.

## I cannot publish an opportunity

Only Organization accounts can publish. The organization must be verified and active. Publishing may also be temporarily blocked when too many contributions are waiting for review. Reviewing them unlocks publishing.

## I chose the wrong account type

The account type cannot be changed after registration. Create a new account with the correct type.

## I cannot find the opportunity I want

Use the Opportunities page and search by title or description. You can also filter by category, skill, wilaya, municipality, or open positions. Keyword search does not cover organization names.

## I cannot edit my contribution

A submission can be edited only while it is a draft or after it is rejected. Once submitted, it cannot be edited until the organization rejects it.

## I cannot upload a file

Evidence can be JPEG, PNG, or WebP images and PDF files up to 5 MB each. Video is added as a YouTube or Google Drive link. Up to 50 evidence items are allowed.

## The platform asks for consent

When evidence includes images or video showing people, consent must be confirmed before submitting.

## The platform is slow at first

The platform may be slow at first after a period of inactivity while it starts up. Try again after a short wait.

## I forgot my password

Use the forgot-password link on the sign-in page. A password-reset message is sent to your email.

## I did not receive the verification email

Check your spam folder and make sure the email address is correct. You can request the verification message again from the platform.

## The organization does not see my contact details

Organizations do not see a volunteer's email or phone. Use the organization's public contact details or the "Email the organization" link on the Submit Contribution page.

## Common Questions

**Why is My Tasks empty?**
Because no organization has accepted you yet, or because the accepted task is not active.

**Why can't I see my Verified Experience?**
The contribution may still be under review, or the experiences setting may be off in your public profile settings.

**Why can't I publish?**
The account may not be an Organization account, may not be verified or active, or may have too many contributions waiting for review.

**Why can't I apply?**
You may not be signed in as a volunteer, the deadline may have passed, or the position may be full.

**Why can't I edit my submission?**
It is no longer a draft or rejected. Only drafts and rejected submissions can be edited.
