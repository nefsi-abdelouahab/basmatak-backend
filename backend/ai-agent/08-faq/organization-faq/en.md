# Organization FAQ

## Getting Started
**How can my association join the platform?**
Create an account and choose the Organization account type at registration.

**Can I change a volunteer account into an organization account?**
No. The account type cannot be changed after registration. A new account is needed.

**Can several people manage the same organization account?**
The platform does not use an organization members model in its current design.

## Publishing Opportunities
**How do I publish an opportunity?**
Sign in with an Organization account, open the opportunity creation page, describe the activity, tasks, required skills, and number of volunteers, then publish.

**What should an opportunity include?**
A clear title, a description of the activity and tasks, the required skills, and the number of volunteers needed. The exact form fields are not confirmed here, so check the creation page.

**Can one opportunity accept several volunteers?**
Yes.

**Can the AI assistant create an opportunity for me?**
Not at the moment. The assistant answers questions and explains the platform. Create opportunities from the platform itself.

## Applications and Selection
**Where do I see volunteers who applied?**
In the applications for your opportunity.

**How do I choose volunteers?**
Review the applicants and select the ones that fit the needs and required skills.

**Does an application automatically make someone a participant?**
No. Only selected volunteers move on to carry out the task.

## Contributions and Verification
**How do I verify a volunteer's contribution?**
Review the submitted description and evidence. If the work was actually completed, confirm it. You can also add an evaluation.

**Can a volunteer verify their own work?**
No. Verification comes from the organization.

**What happens after I confirm a contribution?**
It becomes a Verified Experience on the volunteer's public profile.

**What if a contribution is not accurate?**
I do not have confirmed information about rejection or dispute procedures. An unconfirmed contribution does not become a Verified Experience.

## Finding Volunteers
**How can I find volunteers with specific skills, such as design?**
Use the Discover Talents page to explore public volunteer profiles and their verified experiences.

**Can I message volunteers?**
No. A messaging system is not part of the first version.
