# Opportunity Details Page

## What It Is
The page that shows the full details of one opportunity. Users reach it after choosing an opportunity from the Opportunities page.

## What It Helps You Understand
- The need the organization wants to fulfill.
- The nature of the task.
- The required skills.
- The application option for volunteers.

## Who Can Use It
- Visitors can view the full details but cannot apply.
- Volunteers can view the details and apply.

## Common Questions
**Where do I see the details of an opportunity?**
Open it from the Opportunities page to reach Opportunity Details.

**Can I see details without signing in?**
Yes. Visitors can view details but cannot apply.

**How do I apply?**
Sign in with a Volunteer account, open the opportunity details, and send an application.
