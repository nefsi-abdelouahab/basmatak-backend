# Opportunities Page

## What It Is
The page for exploring volunteering opportunities. Its purpose is to help users discover opportunities that match their skills and interests.

## Who Can Use It
- Visitors can explore opportunities.
- Volunteers can explore opportunities and then apply.
- Organizations can view opportunities.

## What You Can Do
- Browse published opportunities.
- Open an opportunity to see its full details.

## Note
The exact search and filter options are not confirmed in this knowledge base. Do not state specific filters.

## Common Questions
**Where do I find volunteering opportunities?**
On the Opportunities page.

**Do I need an account to browse opportunities?**
No. Visitors can view opportunities. To apply you need a Volunteer account.

**Can I filter opportunities by skill?**
The platform is designed around matching skills with needs, but I do not have confirmed details about the exact filters.
