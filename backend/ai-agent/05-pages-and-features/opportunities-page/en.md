# Opportunities Page

## What It Is

The page for exploring volunteering opportunities. Its goal is to help users find opportunities that match their skills and interests.

## Who Can Use It

- Visitors can explore published opportunities.
- Volunteers can explore opportunities and apply.
- Organizations can view published opportunities.

## What You Can Do

- Browse published opportunities.
- Open an opportunity to read its full details.
- Search by keyword.
- Filter and sort the list.

## Search

The keyword search covers the opportunity title and description only. It does not search by organization name.

## Filters

The page supports filters for:

- Category.
- Skill.
- Wilaya.
- Municipality.
- Open positions.
- Near me, for a signed-in volunteer.

The format option narrows the results already loaded on the page. It does not request new results.

## Sorting

Results can be sorted by newest, oldest, application deadline, or start date.

## Recommended Opportunities

A signed-in volunteer can see a recommended feed based on skills, location, and recency.

## Common Questions

**Where do I find volunteering opportunities?**
On the Opportunities page.

**Do I need an account to browse opportunities?**
No. Visitors can view opportunities. To apply, you need a Volunteer account.

**Can I filter opportunities by skill?**
Yes. The page supports a skill filter.

**Can I search by organization name?**
No. Keyword search covers the title and description only.

**Can I see only opportunities with open positions?**
Yes. Use the open positions filter.

**What does the format option do?**
It narrows the results already loaded on the page. It does not request new results.
