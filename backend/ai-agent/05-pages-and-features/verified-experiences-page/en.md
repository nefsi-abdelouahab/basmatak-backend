# Verified Experiences Page

## What It Is
The page that shows the experiences that were verified and approved. It reflects the main value of the platform.

## What Verified Experiences Show
- The volunteering activity.
- The organization that ran the activity.
- The nature of the volunteer's contribution.
- The linked evidence.
- The verification status.
- The evaluation, when available.

## Where They Appear
On the volunteer's public profile. Public verified experiences can also be viewed by visitors.

## Common Questions
**Where can I see my verified experiences?**
On the Verified Experiences page and on your public profile.

**Why don't I see any verified experiences yet?**
A contribution becomes a Verified Experience only after the organization reviews and confirms it.

**Can visitors see them?**
Public verified experiences can be viewed by visitors.
