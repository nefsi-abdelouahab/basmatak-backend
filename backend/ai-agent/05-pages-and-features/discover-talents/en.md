# Discover Talents Page

## What It Is
A page for discovering volunteers and talents on the platform. It is one of the important pages in the project's idea.

## Logic
People are discovered by their skills and experience, not ranked by popularity. There are no likes, followers, or popularity rankings.

## Who It Helps
- Organizations looking for volunteers with specific skills (for example design).
- Visitors who want to see public talents and public volunteer profiles.

## Privacy
Only public data is shown. Private user data is not shown.

## Common Questions
**What is Discover Talents?**
A page to find volunteers by their skills and verified experiences.

**Is it ranked by popularity?**
No. There is no popularity ranking.

**Can I see private information about volunteers?**
No. Only public profile content is shown.

**How can an organization find a volunteer with design skills?**
Use the Discover Talents page to explore public volunteer profiles and their verified experiences.
