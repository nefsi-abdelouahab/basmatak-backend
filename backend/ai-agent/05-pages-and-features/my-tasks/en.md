# My Tasks Page

## What It Is
The page for the tasks and participations a volunteer is working on after being selected. It connects acceptance of the application with carrying out the contribution.

## Who Uses It
Volunteers, after signing in.

## What It Is For
- Seeing the tasks you were selected for.
- Moving on to submit your contribution when the work is done.

## Common Questions
**Where do I find the tasks I was accepted for?**
On the My Tasks page.

**What do I do when I finish a task?**
Submit your contribution with a description and evidence from the Submit Contribution page.

**Why is My Tasks empty?**
Tasks appear after an organization selects you for an opportunity. Applying alone does not create a task.
