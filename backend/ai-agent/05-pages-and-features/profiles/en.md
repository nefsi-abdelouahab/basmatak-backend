# Profiles: My Profile and Public Profile

## Public Profile
The profile that others can explore. Its key content is the Verified Experiences the volunteer has earned, so it works as a way to show the actual work the person did.

## My Profile
The user's own profile, where they manage their personal information and profile content.

## Volunteer Profile
A volunteer can:
- Show personal and professional information.
- Present their skills.
- Build a record that reflects actual contributions.

## Organization Profile
An organization has its own profile that describes it and its opportunities.

## Public Settings
The platform has volunteer public settings. The exact options are not confirmed in this knowledge base. If asked what can be hidden or shown, say that exact options are not confirmed.

## Skills vs Proof
Skills listed on a profile are claims. Proof comes from Verified Experiences.

## Common Questions
**What is the difference between My Profile and Public Profile?**
My Profile is where you manage your own information. Public Profile is what others see.

**Does my public profile show my verified experiences?**
Yes, that is its main content.

**Can I hide some of my profile information?**
I do not have confirmed details about public visibility options.

**Can I change my account type from Volunteer to Organization?**
No. The account type is chosen at registration and cannot be changed. A new account is needed.
