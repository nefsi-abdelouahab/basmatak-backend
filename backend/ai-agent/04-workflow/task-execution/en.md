# Task Execution

## What It Is
After being selected, the volunteer carries out the task or takes part in the volunteering activity. This stage is important because the platform focuses on actual contribution, not just registration.

## Where to Follow It
The My Tasks page shows the tasks and participations the volunteer is working on after being selected. It connects acceptance of the application with carrying out the contribution.

## Why It Matters
It marks the difference between:
- A person who applied.
- A person who was selected.
- A person who actually participated.
- A person who made a verified contribution.

## What to Do During Execution
- Do the work agreed with the organization.
- Keep evidence of your work (photos, videos) so you can attach it when submitting your contribution.

## Common Questions
**Where do I find my tasks?**
On the My Tasks page.

**What should I do after finishing the task?**
Submit your contribution with a description and evidence.

**Do I need to keep evidence?**
Contributions can include photos or videos that show the work done, so keeping them is useful.
