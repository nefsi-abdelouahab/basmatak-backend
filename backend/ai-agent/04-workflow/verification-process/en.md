# Verification Process

## What It Is
Verification is the organization's confirmation that a submitted contribution was actually completed. It is the most important feature of Basmatak Tatatoua.

## Rules
- A volunteer cannot turn their own contribution into a Verified Experience.
- The organization responsible for the activity reviews and confirms the contribution.
- A submission is not a Verified Experience. Verification is what makes it one.
- The organization can also give an evaluation.

## For Organizations
1. Open the contributions submitted by volunteers for your opportunity.
2. Review the description and evidence.
3. If the work was actually completed, confirm it.
4. You can add an evaluation.

## Result
A confirmed contribution becomes a Verified Experience and can appear on the volunteer's public profile, showing:
- The volunteering activity.
- The organization that ran it.
- The nature of the contribution.
- The linked evidence.
- The verification status.
- The evaluation, when available.

## Note
Details about other verifying parties (for example a beneficiary) and how each type is approved are not confirmed in this knowledge base.

## Common Questions
**Who verifies my contribution?**
The organization responsible for the activity.

**Can I verify my own contribution?**
No.

**What happens after verification?**
It becomes a Verified Experience on your public profile.

**What if the organization does not confirm my contribution?**
I do not have confirmed information about this case. You can contact the organization directly.
