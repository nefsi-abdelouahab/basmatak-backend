# Applying to an Opportunity

## Who Can Apply
Only signed-in users with a Volunteer account. Visitors can view opportunity details but cannot apply. Organization accounts do not apply to opportunities.

## Steps
1. Sign in with a Volunteer account.
2. Open the Opportunities page and explore opportunities.
3. Open Opportunity Details to understand the need, the task, and the required skills.
4. Send an application to join the opportunity.
5. Follow your application on the Applications page.

## Important
- An application is a request. It does not make you a participant.
- The organization reviews applications and decides whom to select.
- Choose opportunities that fit your skills, since organizations select based on the opportunity's needs and applicants' skills.

## If You Are Not Signed In
You can view the opportunity, but to apply you need to create a Volunteer account and sign in.

## Common Questions
**How do I apply to an opportunity?**
Sign in as a volunteer, open the opportunity details, and send an application.

**Can I apply without an account?**
No. You can only view the opportunity. Applying requires a Volunteer account.

**Can an organization account apply?**
No. Applying is a volunteer action.

**Where can I follow my application?**
On the Applications page.

**Am I accepted automatically after applying?**
No. The organization reviews applications and selects volunteers.

**What are the possible statuses of an application?**
I do not have confirmed information about the exact status names. Check the Applications page.
