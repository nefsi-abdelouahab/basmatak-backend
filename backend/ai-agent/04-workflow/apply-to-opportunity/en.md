# Applying to an Opportunity

## Who Can Apply

Only signed-in users with a Volunteer account can apply. Visitors can read the opportunity but cannot apply. Organization accounts cannot apply.

## Steps

1. Sign in with a Volunteer account.
2. Open the Opportunities page and choose an opportunity.
3. Read the opportunity details and choose a specific position.
4. Send an application with an optional cover message.
5. Follow the application on the Applications page.

## Requirements

- The opportunity must be published and must still accept applications.
- The application deadline, when set, must not have passed.
- The chosen position must have an open seat.
- A volunteer can have only one live application per position. A live application is pending, under review, or accepted.
- The cover message can be up to 2000 characters. It cannot be edited after applying.

## After Applying

The application starts as pending. The organization may move it to under review and then accept or reject it. Applying does not make the volunteer a participant, and it does not create a task.

## Withdrawing

A volunteer can withdraw a pending or under-review application. An accepted application can also be withdrawn before the participation is completed; this frees the seat.

## Common Questions

**How do I apply to an opportunity?**
Sign in as a volunteer, open the opportunity, choose a position, and send an application.

**Can I apply without an account?**
No. You can read the opportunity, but applying requires a Volunteer account.

**Can an organization account apply?**
No. Applying is a volunteer action.

**Can I apply to several positions in the same opportunity?**
You can apply to different positions, but only one live application per position is allowed.

**Can I change my cover message later?**
No. The cover message is not editable after applying.

**Does applying guarantee acceptance?**
No. The organization reviews applications and decides.
