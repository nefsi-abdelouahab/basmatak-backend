# Volunteer guide

Basmatak connects volunteers with published opportunities. This guide explains the first steps.

## Applying

Choose an open position, review the requirements, and apply before the deadline. One active application is allowed per position.

### Evidence

- Submit a clear photo of the completed work.
- Wait for the organization to review the evidence.

## Verification

The organization confirms the contribution after reviewing the evidence. Only confirmed work becomes a verified experience.
