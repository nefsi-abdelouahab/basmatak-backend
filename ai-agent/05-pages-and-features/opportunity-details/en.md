# Opportunity Details Page

## What It Is

The page that shows the full details of one opportunity. Users reach it after choosing an opportunity from the Opportunities page.

## What It Helps You Understand

- The need the organization wants to fulfill.
- The activity and its description.
- The positions and how many volunteers each one needs.
- The required skills, deliverables, and start time for each position.
- The location, mode, dates, duration, and application deadline.
- The organization that published it.
- Whether the organization is verified.

## What the Page Shows

- The opportunity title, description, category, and location.
- The mode: on-site, remote, or hybrid.
- The start date, end date, duration, prerequisites, and application deadline.
- A cover image when available.
- The organization's public identity and verified badge.
- The positions with their required and filled numbers, skills, deliverables, and start times.

## Who Can Use It

- Visitors can read the full details but cannot apply.
- Volunteers can read the details and apply to a position.
- Organizations can view the details.

## Applying

A volunteer chooses a position with an open seat and sends an application before the deadline. The application can include an optional cover message.

## Common Questions

**Where do I see the details of an opportunity?**
Open it from the Opportunities page.

**Can I see the details without signing in?**
Yes. Visitors can read the details but cannot apply.

**How do I apply?**
Sign in with a Volunteer account, open the opportunity, choose a position, and send an application.

**What does the verified badge mean?**
It means the organization completed the platform's verification.

**Can I apply if the position is full?**
No. A full position has no open seat.
