# My Tasks Page

## What It Is

The page for the tasks and participations a volunteer works on after being accepted. It connects an accepted application to the contribution.

## Who Uses It

Volunteers, after signing in.

## What It Shows

- The tasks the volunteer was accepted for.
- The opportunity, position, schedule, and location.
- The participation state.
- A link to submit a contribution when the work is done.

## Participation States

- Accepted: the volunteer was accepted and the task has not started.
- Active: the opportunity started and the work is in progress.
- Completed: the contribution was verified.
- Withdrawn: the volunteer withdrew.
- Removed: the organization removed the participant with a reason.

## What a Volunteer Can Do

- Open a task to read its details.
- Withdraw before the participation is completed.
- Submit a contribution with a description and evidence.

## Common Questions

**Where do I find the tasks I was accepted for?**
On the My Tasks page.

**What do I do when I finish a task?**
Submit your contribution with a description and evidence from the Submit Contribution page.

**Why is My Tasks empty?**
Tasks appear after an organization accepts you. Applying alone does not create a task.

**When does a task become active?**
When the opportunity starts. If a start date is set, the platform starts it automatically.

**Can I withdraw a task?**
Yes, before the participation is completed. This frees the seat.

**Can the organization remove me?**
Yes, with a reason.
