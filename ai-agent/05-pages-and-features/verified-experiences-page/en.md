# Verified Experiences Page

## What It Is

The page that shows experiences that were verified and confirmed. It reflects the platform's core value.

## What a Verified Experience Shows

- The volunteering activity.
- The organization that ran the activity.
- The nature of the volunteer's contribution.
- The evidence linked to it.
- The verification status.
- The organization's review, when available.

## Where It Appears

On the volunteer's public profile. Visitors can also view public Verified Experiences.

## Verification Code

Each Verified Experience has a verification code. Anyone can check it on the public verification page at /verify/<code>. A PDF attestation can be generated.

## Common Questions

**Where can I see my Verified Experiences?**
On the Verified Experiences page and on your public profile.

**Why don't I see any Verified Experiences yet?**
A contribution becomes a Verified Experience only after the organization reviews and confirms it.

**Can visitors see them?**
Visitors can view public Verified Experiences.

**Can I share proof of a Verified Experience?**
Yes. You can share its verification code, which can be checked on the public verification page, or a PDF attestation.
