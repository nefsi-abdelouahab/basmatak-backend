# Applications Page

## What It Is

The page where a volunteer follows the applications they sent to opportunities and the state of each one.

## Who Uses It

Volunteers, after signing in.

## What It Shows

- The opportunities and positions the volunteer applied to.
- The state of each application.
- The cover message sent with the application.
- The rejection reason when an application is rejected.

## Application States

- Pending: the application was sent and is waiting for the organization.
- Under review: the organization is reviewing the application.
- Accepted: the volunteer was accepted and a participation was created.
- Rejected: the organization rejected the application with a reason.
- Withdrawn: the volunteer withdrew the application.

## What a Volunteer Can Do

- Open an application to read its details.
- Withdraw a pending or under-review application.
- Withdraw an accepted application before the participation is completed; this frees the seat.
- Go to My Tasks when an application is accepted.

## Important

An application is a request to join. It does not make the volunteer a participant. The organization reviews applications and decides.

## Common Questions

**Where can I follow my applications?**
On the Applications page.

**What do the states mean?**
Pending means waiting, under review means the organization is reviewing, accepted means you were accepted, rejected means the organization declined with a reason, and withdrawn means you withdrew.

**I applied and nothing happened. What now?**
The organization reviews applications and decides. You can follow the application on the Applications page.

**Can I withdraw an application?**
Yes, while it is pending or under review, and also after acceptance before the participation is completed.

**Can I change my cover message?**
No. It is not editable after applying.
