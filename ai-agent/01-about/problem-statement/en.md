# Problems the Platform Solves

Basmatak Tatatoua addresses four problems in the volunteering ecosystem.

## 1. Difficulty finding volunteering opportunities
Young people may have different skills, but they often do not know which associations need them, or they have no organized way to reach suitable opportunities.

## 2. Difficulty for associations in finding suitable volunteers
Associations may need people with skills in:
- Programming and web development.
- Design and graphics.
- Photography and video editing.
- Marketing and social media management.
- Translation and content writing.
- Organization and event management.
- Other fields.

Reaching the right people and coordinating their contributions can be hard.

## 3. Lack of real documentation of volunteering experience
In many cases, a volunteering experience ends with a participation certificate or proof of attendance, without explaining what work the person actually did.

## 4. Difficulty proving the experience gained from volunteering
Young people can gain real practical experience through volunteering, but they do not always have an organized way to present and prove it.

## The Platform's Answer
Basmatak Tatatoua links skills to needs, then documents the contribution after it is completed.

## Common Questions
**Why do we need this platform?**
Because volunteering experience is usually hard to find, hard to coordinate, and hard to prove.

**What is wrong with a participation certificate?**
It usually shows attendance only, not what the person actually did or which skills they used.
