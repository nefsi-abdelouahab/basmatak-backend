# Platform Overview: What is Basmatak Tatatoua?

## Definition
Basmatak Tatatoua (Arabic: بصمتك تطوع, meaning "Your Volunteering Footprint") is a digital platform that connects young volunteers with associations and organizations that need their skills to carry out volunteering activities and projects.

## The Core Idea
The platform is not only about publishing volunteering opportunities or counting volunteering hours. Its main idea is to turn real volunteering contributions into documented experiences that can be proven and referred to later.

Instead of only showing that a person attended a volunteering activity, the platform documents:
- What the volunteer did.
- Which task they completed.
- Which skills they used.
- What their actual contribution was.
- Which organization confirmed this contribution.
- What evidence proves their participation.

This documented experience becomes part of the volunteer's profile and serves as practical proof of their experience and contributions.

## The Core Equation
Skill + Volunteering Opportunity + Actual Contribution + Organization Confirmation = Verified Volunteering Experience.

## Core Value Proposition
"From Volunteering to Verified Experience": moving from simply participating in volunteering activities to building a real, documented record of experiences and contributions.

## Who Uses the Platform
There are two account types, plus visitors who browse without an account:
- Volunteer: looks for opportunities, applies, carries out tasks, and submits contributions.
- Organization: an association or organization that publishes opportunities, selects volunteers, and confirms contributions.
- Visitor: not an account type. Anyone not signed in who explores public opportunities, public profiles, and public verified experiences.

## Common Questions
**What is Basmatak Tatatoua?**
A platform that links volunteers' skills to organizations' needs and documents real volunteering contributions as verified experiences.

**Is it a social network?**
No. It is intentionally not a social network. It focuses on real contributions and verified experience, not popularity.

**What does "Verified Experience" mean?**
A volunteering contribution that the responsible organization has reviewed and confirmed as actually completed.

**Is it only for volunteers?**
No. It serves volunteers and organizations through accounts, and lets anyone browse public opportunities and talents without an account.
