# The Organization Behind the Platform

## Who
Roeya Association for Media and Audiovisual Production (Arabic: جمعية رؤية للإعلام والإنتاج السمعي البصري). In the project context it is referred to as Roeya.

## Details
- Location: Timimoun, Algeria.
- Founded: May 31, 2023.
- Field: Media and audiovisual production.

## Activities Associated with the Association
- Media and audiovisual promotion.
- Training and capacity building in the media field.
- Documenting oral memory.
- Training young people.
- Producing audiovisual content.
