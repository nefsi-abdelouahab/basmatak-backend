# Core Principles of Basmatak Tatatoua

1. Actual contributions matter more than participation counts.
2. Verified experience is more valuable than popularity.
3. Organizations must confirm contributions. A volunteer cannot verify their own contribution.
4. Volunteers should have a documented record of their work.
5. Skills listed on a profile are different from experience proven through a verified contribution.
6. The platform is not a traditional social network.
7. Public data and private user data are kept separate.

## The Difference Between Steps
These are different things in the platform:
- A person who applied to an opportunity.
- A person who was selected.
- A person who actually participated.
- A person whose contribution was verified.

Sending an application does not make someone a participant. Sending a contribution does not make it a Verified Experience. Verification comes from the organization.

## Common Questions
**Is the platform about popularity?**
No. There are no followers, likes, or popularity rankings.

**Who proves that a contribution is real?**
The organization responsible for the activity reviews and confirms it.

**Can a volunteer verify their own work?**
No. Verification must come from the organization.
