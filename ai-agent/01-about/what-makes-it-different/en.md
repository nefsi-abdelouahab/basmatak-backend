# What Makes Basmatak Tatatoua Different

## Not Another Social Network

The platform deliberately avoids social-network mechanics. There are no followers, no likes, and no popularity ranking. Value comes from real work, not attention.

## Verification by the Organization

A contribution becomes a Verified Experience only after the responsible organization reviews and confirms it.

## Evidence-Based Records

A volunteer can attach a description and evidence to their contribution: photos, PDF files, and video links. The record then shows what was actually done, not just attendance.

## Skills Linked to Needs

Volunteers present their skills, organizations describe their needs, and the platform connects the two.

## Public Verified Portfolio

Verified experiences can appear on the volunteer's public profile, giving practical proof of experience that is more informative than a list of certificates or hours.

## Talent Discovery Without Popularity

The Discover Talents page lets people find volunteers by skills and verified experience, not by number of likes or followers.

## Comparison

| Traditional volunteering record | Basmatak Tatatoua |
|---|---|
| Attendance or participation certificate | Documented contribution with evidence |
| Shows that you were present | Shows what you did and which skills you used |
| Hard to verify | Confirmed by the organization |
| Often scattered | Collected in one public profile |

## Common Questions

**How is it different from a normal volunteering website?**
It does not only list opportunities. It documents real contributions and verifies them through the organization.

**Why no likes or followers?**
Because likes and followers do not prove that someone did volunteering work.
