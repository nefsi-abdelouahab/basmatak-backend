# Basmatak guides for volunteers and organizations

## Searching and filtering opportunities

The public opportunities feed shows published opportunities from registered organizations. Use the search field to look by title, organization, skill, or place. Use the filters to narrow the list by wilaya, category, required skills, and format. The format can be on-site, remote, or hybrid. Sort the results by newest, oldest, closing soon, or starting soon.

Opportunity cards show the organization, location, required skills, open positions, and application deadline. If no card matches the filters, clear some filters or try different keywords.

## Reading an opportunity before applying

Open the opportunity page to see the full description, positions, prerequisites, deliverables, dates, application deadline, and the publishing organization. A verified organization badge means the organization has completed platform verification. Check the remaining seats and the deadline before starting an application. Applying stops at the application deadline.

## Applying and tracking an application

Sign in with a volunteer account to apply. Choose a position with an open seat and submit the application before the deadline. One active application per position is allowed. Track applications in My Applications. A pending or accepted application can be withdrawn. The organization can accept or reject the application.

## Participating and submitting evidence

An accepted application creates a participation. Accepted participants become active automatically at the opportunity start date. My Tasks lists participations. After completing the work, open the participation and create a submission with a description and evidence for each deliverable. When the evidence includes images or video, confirm that you have consent to share them. The organization reviews the submission and verifies or rejects it. A rejected submission can be edited and submitted again.

## Understanding verification and experience

A verified experience is created only after the organization confirms the submitted work. It is stored with the volunteer's experiences and can be made public individually. The organization's confirmation is what distinguishes a verified experience from a self-reported claim.

## Managing a public profile

Volunteers control their public profile. The profile can be enabled or disabled, and individual sections can be shown or hidden. A verified experience can be made public or kept private. Public visitors can browse public profiles through Discover Talents. Private contact details are not shown publicly. Review the profile after changing visibility settings.

## Publishing as an organization

Register an organization account and complete organization verification. An organization cannot publish until an administrator approves its verification. After approval, create an opportunity with its positions and publish it. Review applications, accept or reject them, and review submitted evidence after the work is completed. Confirming a submission creates the volunteer's verified experience. The public organization directory lists verified, active organizations.
