# Submission vs Verified Experience

## Submission (Contribution)

After carrying out the work, the volunteer submits a contribution. It includes a title (up to 200 characters), a description (up to 5000 characters), and evidence: up to 50 items. Evidence can be JPEG, PNG, or WebP images and PDF files up to 5 MB each, plus links to YouTube or Google Drive videos. A submission is the volunteer's own claim, supported by evidence. It is not yet proof.

## Verified Experience

After the responsible organization reviews the contribution and confirms it was actually completed, it becomes a Verified Experience. It can then appear on the volunteer's public profile.

## Key Rules

- A submission does not equal a Verified Experience.
- A volunteer cannot turn their own contribution into a Verified Experience.
- Only draft or rejected submissions can be edited. Editing a rejected one returns it to draft, and it can be submitted again.
- A submission cannot be deleted.
- When evidence includes images or video, the volunteer must confirm consent before submitting.
- The organization can review the contribution with a rating from 1 to 5 and an optional comment.

## Example

A volunteer with design skills worked with an association on a visual identity for an activity. Instead of only recording that they attended, they submit a contribution showing the designs they made, the nature of their work, and visual evidence. Once the association confirms it, it becomes a Verified Experience.

## Comparison

| | Submission | Verified Experience |
|---|---|---|
| Created by | The volunteer | Result of the organization's confirmation |
| Status | Draft, submitted, under review, approved, or rejected | Confirmed |
| Visible as proof | No | Yes |
| Requires organization action | Yes, to be reviewed | Already confirmed |

## Common Questions

**I finished my task. What do I do now?**
Submit your contribution with a description and evidence from the Submit Contribution page.

**Is my contribution a Verified Experience as soon as I submit it?**
No. It becomes one only after the organization reviews and confirms it.

**Who verifies my contribution?**
The organization responsible for the activity.

**Why can't I verify my own work?**
Because the value of a Verified Experience comes from independent confirmation by the organization.

**Can I edit or delete my contribution?**
You can edit it only while it is a draft or after it is rejected. It cannot be deleted.
