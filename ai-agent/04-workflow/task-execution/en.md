# Task Execution

## What It Is

After being accepted, the volunteer carries out the task or takes part in the volunteering activity. This stage matters because the platform focuses on the actual contribution, not only on registration.

## Where to Follow It

The My Tasks page shows the tasks and participations the volunteer is working on after acceptance. It connects the accepted application to the contribution.

## Participation Statuses

A participation can be accepted, active, completed, withdrawn, or removed.

- Accepted: the volunteer was accepted and the task has not started yet.
- Active: the opportunity has started and the volunteer is carrying out the task.
- Completed: the contribution was verified.
- Withdrawn: the volunteer withdrew.
- Removed: the organization removed the participant with a reason.

## Automatic Start

When an opportunity has a start date, the platform starts it automatically at that date and activates accepted participants.

## What to Do During the Task

- Carry out the work agreed with the organization.
- Keep evidence of your work, such as photos, PDF files, and video links, so you can attach it when you submit your contribution.

## Withdrawing or Removal

A volunteer can withdraw before the participation is completed; this frees the seat. The organization can remove a participant with a reason, but removal does not free the seat.

## Common Questions

**Where do I find my tasks?**
On the My Tasks page.

**What do I do after finishing the task?**
Submit your contribution with a description and evidence.

**Why is My Tasks empty?**
Tasks appear after an organization accepts you. Applying alone does not create a task.

**Do I need to keep evidence?**
Yes, it is useful. Contributions can include images, PDF files, and video links showing the completed work.

**Can I withdraw after being accepted?**
Yes, before the participation is completed.

**Can the organization remove me?**
Yes, with a reason.
