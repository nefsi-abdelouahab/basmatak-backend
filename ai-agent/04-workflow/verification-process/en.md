# The Verification Process

## What It Is

Verification is the organization's confirmation that the submitted contribution was actually carried out. It is the most important feature of Basmatak Tatatoua.

## The Rules

- A volunteer cannot turn their own contribution into a Verified Experience.
- The organization responsible for the activity reviews the contribution and confirms it.
- A submission is not a Verified Experience. Verification is what makes it one.
- The organization can add notes of up to 2000 characters when verifying.
- A rejection requires a reason.
- Verification can include a review of the volunteer's work: a rating from 1 to 5 and an optional comment of up to 2000 characters.

## For Organizations

1. Open the contributions submitted for your opportunity.
2. Review the description and evidence.
3. If the work was actually done, verify it and optionally add notes of up to 2000 characters.
4. If it was not done, reject it with a reason.
5. Optionally write a review of the contribution.

## The Result

A verified contribution becomes a Verified Experience and can appear on the volunteer's public profile. It shows:

- The volunteering activity.
- The organization that ran it.
- The nature of the contribution.
- The evidence linked to it.
- The verification status.
- The organization's review, when available.
- Organization notes, when available, shown to the volunteer and not publicly.

## Organization Notes

Verification may include notes from the organization, up to 2000 characters. The volunteer sees them as **Organization notes** on the verified experience card. Notes are not shown publicly.

## Verification Code and Attestation

Each Verified Experience gets a verification code. Anyone can check it on the public verification page at /verify/<code>. A PDF attestation can be generated.

## Common Questions

**What does verification mean?**
Verification is the organization's confirmation that the submitted contribution was actually carried out.

**Who verifies a contribution?**
Only the organization responsible for the activity.

**Can a volunteer verify their own contribution?**
No.

**What happens if the contribution is rejected?**
The volunteer can edit it, which returns it to draft, and submit it again.

**Can the organization verify without reviewing?**
The organization can verify a submitted or under-review contribution. Verification confirms that the work was actually done.

**Can the organization leave notes when verifying?**
Yes. The organization can add notes of up to 2000 characters when it verifies a contribution.

**Where do I see the organization's notes?**
On the verified experience card, under **Organization notes**. They are visible to you and are not shown publicly.

**Can I get a PDF attestation?**
Yes. A PDF attestation can be generated for a Verified Experience.
