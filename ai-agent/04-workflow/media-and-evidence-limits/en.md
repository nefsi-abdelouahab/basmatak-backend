# Media and Evidence Limits

## Evidence for a Contribution

Evidence supports the organization's review of a contribution. It does not replace the organization's confirmation.

## Accepted Files

- JPEG images.
- PNG images.
- WebP images.
- PDF documents.
- Each file can be up to 5 MB.
- Up to 50 evidence items per submission.

## Video

Video is never uploaded. It is added as a link from YouTube or Google Drive only.

## Deliverables

Every deliverable in the position must be covered by evidence before the contribution can be submitted. The volunteer confirms this when submitting.

## Consent

When evidence includes images or video showing people, the volunteer must confirm consent before submitting.

## Privacy

Uploaded evidence files are private by default. They are not shown publicly unless the platform makes a specific public media item available.

## Other Media

The platform also uses media for avatars, organization logos and covers, verification documents, portfolio items, opportunity covers, and organization documents. Each purpose has its own rules.

## Common Questions

**What file types can I attach as evidence?**
JPEG, PNG, or WebP images and PDF documents.

**What is the maximum size of one file?**
5 MB.

**How many evidence items can I attach?**
Up to 50 per submission.

**Can I upload a video?**
No. Video is added as a YouTube or Google Drive link.

**Can I use a Google Drive link?**
The allowed video link hosts are YouTube and Google Drive. Other hosts are rejected.

**Is my evidence public?**
No. Uploaded evidence is private by default.

**Do I need consent?**
Yes, when the evidence includes images or video showing people.
