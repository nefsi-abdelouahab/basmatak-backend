# Opportunity Statuses

## Why Statuses Matter

Every opportunity has a status that shows where it is in its life. Only one status is public.

## The Six Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Public |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | The organization is still preparing it | No |
| Published | It is open and accepting applications while seats are available | Yes |
| In progress | The start date has arrived and the work is under way | No |
| Completed | The work is finished and the organization marked it complete | No |
| Closed | The organization ended it normally | No |
| Cancelled | The organization ended it before completion | No |

## Allowed Transitions

- Draft to Published.
- Published to In progress.
- In progress to Completed.
- Published or In progress to Closed.
- Published or In progress to Cancelled.

No other transitions are allowed. For example, a completed opportunity cannot return to published, and a closed opportunity cannot return to in progress.

## Automatic Start

When an opportunity has a start date, the platform moves it from published to in progress automatically at that date and activates accepted participants.

## What Each Status Means for Volunteers

- Draft: not visible, and no applications are possible.
- Published: visible, and applications are possible while seats are open.
- In progress: no longer in the public feed, and accepted volunteers carry out the work.
- Completed: the work is finished.
- Closed: no longer accepting applications.
- Cancelled: no longer taking place.

## Common Questions

**Which statuses appear in the public feed?**
Only published.

**Can a draft opportunity be edited?**
Yes. The full profile can be edited while it is a draft.

**Can a published opportunity return to draft?**
No. The allowed transitions do not include that.

**When does an opportunity move to in progress?**
When its start date arrives, if one is set.

**What is the difference between closed and cancelled?**
Both end the opportunity. Closed ends it normally; cancelled ends it before completion.

**Can a completed opportunity be published again?**
No. A completed opportunity cannot return to published. The organization can duplicate it as a new draft instead.
