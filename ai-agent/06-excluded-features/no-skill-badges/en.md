# No Skill Badges

## The Decision
Skill badges were deliberately excluded from the first version of the platform.

## Why
The goal is to avoid turning skills into symbols or decorative marks that do not prove the person really has the skill. There is a difference between listing a skill on a profile and proving practical experience through a verified contribution.

## What Replaces Badges
Real experience is shown through Verified Experiences: a documented contribution, with evidence, confirmed by an organization.

## Common Questions
**Are there badges or awards for skills?**
No. Skill badges are not part of the platform.

**How can I show that I am good at a skill?**
By completing contributions where you used that skill and getting them verified by organizations. They appear on your public profile.

**Can I list skills on my profile?**
Yes. Volunteers can present their skills. Listing a skill is a claim, while verified experience is the proof.
