# Organization FAQ

## Getting Started

**How can my association join the platform?**
Create an account and choose the Organization account type at registration.

**Can I change a volunteer account into an organization account?**
No. The account type cannot be changed after registration. A new account is needed.

**Can several people manage the same organization account?**
The platform does not use an organization members model in its current design.

**When can my organization publish opportunities?**
After the organization is verified and active.

## Publishing Opportunities

**How do I publish an opportunity?**
Sign in with an Organization account, create the opportunity as a draft, add its positions and requirements, then publish.

**What should an opportunity include?**
A clear title and description, a category and location, and one or more positions. Each position has a title, a description, a required number of volunteers, required skills, deliverables, and an optional start time.

**Can one opportunity accept several volunteers?**
Yes. Each position has its own required number of volunteers.

**Why can't my organization publish a new opportunity?**
The account may not be verified or active, or too many contributions may be waiting for review. Reviewing pending contributions unlocks publishing.

**Can I edit an opportunity after publishing?**
Only a limited set of fields, and only while no participant has been accepted.

## Applications and Participants

**Where do I see volunteers who applied?**
On the Applications page in the organization workspace.

**How do I accept or reject an application?**
Open the application and accept or reject it. A rejection requires a reason.

**Can I remove a participant?**
Yes, with a reason. Removal does not free the seat.

**Does the organization see a volunteer's email or phone?**
No. Organizations do not see a volunteer's email or phone.

## Contributions and Verification

**How do I verify a contribution?**
Open the submitted contribution, review the description and evidence, then verify it with optional notes or reject it with a reason.

**Can I write a review?**
Yes. You can give a rating from 1 to 5 with an optional comment of up to 2000 characters.

**What happens after I verify a contribution?**
It becomes a Verified Experience for the volunteer and can appear on the volunteer's public profile.

## Account and Notifications

**Can I delete the organization account?**
Yes. Account deletion is available and is a soft delete that ends all sessions.

**How do I receive notifications?**
Through the in-app notification center. Email is used only for account verification and password reset.

**Can Basma create an opportunity for me?**
Not at the moment. Basma explains the platform and can search public information, but it does not perform actions.

## Common Questions

**Is there a limit on how many opportunities I can publish?**
There is no confirmed numeric limit. Publishing may be temporarily blocked when too many contributions are waiting for review.

**Can I add team members to my organization account?**
No. The platform does not use an organization members model.

**Can I contact a volunteer directly?**
There is no messaging. You can use rejection or removal reasons, verification notes, review comments, and status notifications. You cannot see the volunteer's email or phone.

**Can I reject a contribution without a reason?**
No. A rejection requires a reason.
